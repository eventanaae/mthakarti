Human–AI Collaboration: Key Ideas (التعاون بين الإنسان والذكاء الاصطناعي)

🎯 ذاكر الدرس وحل الكويز مجانًا في مذاكرتي
TermDefinitionExample
Human–AI collaboration (التعاون بين الإنسان والذكاء الاصطناعي)When humans and AI systems work together to complete tasksA doctor uses AI to analyse X-rays but makes the final decision
Decision support system (نظام دعم القرار)An AI system that assists humans by giving recommendations rather than making final decisionsA clinical AI suggesting possible diagnoses for a doctor to review
Explainability (قابلية التفسير)The ability to understand how an AI system reached a decisionA bank explains why an AI rejected a loan application
Accountability (المساءلة)Being responsible for decisions and their outcomes, even when AI is involvedA doctor is accountable for a diagnosis supported by AI
Human judgment (الحكم البشري)Using experience, ethics and context to make informed decisionsA pilot decides to override autopilot in an emergency
Human oversight (الإشراف البشري)Humans monitor and check AI decisions to make sure they are correctA pilot supervising autopilot during a flight
The partnership: AI systems analyse large amounts of data, identify patterns, and generate predictions or recommendations. Humans apply critical thinking, consider ethics and context, make or approve final decisions, and accept responsibility for outcomes.
Key idea: AI supports decision-making, but humans remain accountable for decisions that affect people.
Dubai International Airport: Smart Gates with facial recognition, self-service check-in kiosks and AI luggage scanning do routine work; staff supervise the systems, assist passengers and make final decisions when needed.
