Levels of Human Involvement & Who Decides (مستويات تدخل الإنسان)

🤖 الذكاء الاصطناعي والتكنولوجيا🎓 الصف العاشر عام📌 هيكل الامتحان: L02 Human-AI Collaboration

🎯 ذاكر الدرس وحل الكويز مجانًا في مذاكرتي

The continuum of human involvement (from most human control to least):

  1. Human in control
  2. Human supervises AI
  3. Human approves AI recommendation
  4. AI makes decision with human oversight
  5. Fully automated system (نظام مؤتمت بالكامل)
The higher the risk to people (health, money, safety, law), the more human involvement is needed.
TaskSuitable level
Recommending movies / musicAI – fully automated is acceptable (low risk)
Diagnosing cancer / medical diagnosisHuman + AI – AI suggests, the doctor approves and is accountable
Approving a bank loanHuman + AI – AI recommends, a human checks for fairness
Self-driving vehiclesAI with human oversight – high safety risk
Human oversight is essential when: decisions affect people's health; decisions affect financial wellbeing; decisions have legal consequences; data may be biased; ethical judgement is required.
⚠️ Neural networks predict from patterns – they do not "understand". So an AI predicting a disease should NOT make the final decision alone.
