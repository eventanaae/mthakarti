Neural Networks and Deep Learning (التعلم العميق)

Deep learning (التعلم العميق): a specialised area of machine learning that uses multiple (many) hidden layers of neural networks.
Example: deep learning models in self-driving cars recognise pedestrians, traffic lights and road signs.

Why deep learning? Traditional neural networks with only one or two hidden layers struggle to model highly complex data. Deep learning uses many hidden layers that automatically learn hierarchical representations (تمثيلات هرمية):

  • Early layers → detect simple features (edges)
  • Middle layers → detect shapes and textures
  • Later layers → recognise complete objects or concepts

When is deep learning preferred? Image recognition, natural language processing, speech recognition, autonomous vehicles.

TypeHidden layersBest used for
Single-layer networkNoneSimple linear classification
Multi-layer network1–2Moderate pattern recognition
Deep neural network3 or moreImages, speech, language, autonomous systems
Key idea: as problems become more complex, neural networks need deeper architectures to learn increasingly abstract patterns.
⚠️ Simple tasks like adding 5 + 5 or showing the time do NOT need deep learning. Recognising objects in thousands of images does.
