Activity 1.1 – compare architectures:

FeatureNetwork A (1 hidden)Network B (2 hidden)Network C (4 hidden)
Simple or deep?SimpleMediumDeep
Training timeFastestMediumSlowest
Computational costLowestMediumHighest
Data neededLeastMediumLargest dataset
Risk of overfitting with small dataLowestMediumHighest

Activity 1.2 – Model A (1 hidden layer, 20 neurons) vs Model B (5 hidden layers, 200 neurons):

  • Recognising handwritten digits (simple dataset) → Model A is enough: fast, cheap, less risk of overfitting.
  • Detecting cancer from medical scans (complex dataset) → Model B: complex patterns need a deeper network (with enough data).
  • Judge using: overfitting, training time, computational cost, data requirements.
Model B can perform worse than Model A when the task is simple or the dataset is small: it may overfit, train slowly and cost more.
Activity 1.4 – recommendation systems: on a shopping or streaming site, "Recommended for you" shows items similar to what you viewed. Problems: repeated similar products, some irrelevant suggestions, and poor suggestions for new users (not enough data about them). The hidden layers have learned features like your categories, brands and viewing history.
⚠️ There is always a trade-off between model complexity and computational efficiency.

Which network is most likely to overfit if only a small amount of data is available?

✅ الجواب: The deepest network (most hidden layers)

Complex models memorise small datasets easily.

Model A: 1 hidden layer, 20 neurons. Model B: 5 hidden layers, 200 neurons. Which suits detecting cancer from medical scans (complex dataset)?

✅ الجواب: Model B

Complex medical images need a deeper network.

Which model suits recognising handwritten digits from a simple dataset?

✅ الجواب: Model A (1 hidden layer)

A simple dataset does not need a deep, costly model.
