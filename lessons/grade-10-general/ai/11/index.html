Data Quality and Decision Quality (جودة البيانات وجودة القرار)

🤖 الذكاء الاصطناعي والتكنولوجيا🎓 الصف العاشر عام📌 هيكل الامتحان: L02 Human-AI Collaboration

🎯 ذاكر الدرس وحل الكويز مجانًا في مذاكرتي
AI does not understand data like humans. It identifies statistical patterns learned during training. So the quality of the data decides the quality of the decisions.
Data characteristicImpact on AI decisions
Accurate (دقيقة)Better predictions
Complete (مكتملة)Fewer missing patterns
Representative (ممثِّلة)Fairer decisions
Biased (متحيزة)Unfair outcomes
Outdated (قديمة)Poor recommendations

Activity 2.2 – a hospital AI to detect lung disease from chest X-rays:

Dataset ADataset B
50,000 images; high quality and accurately labelled; adults aged 40–70 only; limited younger patients; one hospital120,000 images; multiple hospitals; different ages and backgrounds; some missing labels / inconsistent quality; needs extra cleaning
✔ Likely more accurate in the short term
✘ Higher risk of bias (not representative)		✔ Likely fairer across patient groups (diverse)
✘ Needs more preparation; label errors can reduce reliability
Judge datasets using: data quality, diversity, risk of bias, preparation required, expected reliability.
⚠️ A bigger dataset is not automatically better – quality and representativeness matter too.
