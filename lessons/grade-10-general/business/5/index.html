Using Payment Tools Safely (الاستخدام الآمن لأدوات الدفع)

💼 الدراسات التجارية🎓 الصف العاشر عام📌 هيكل الامتحان: M1.5 Financial Literacy

🎯 ذاكر الدرس وحل الكويز مجانًا في مذاكرتي

Digital payments are fast, but criminals try to steal money using scams (الاحتيال).

Phishing (التصيّد الاحتيالي): a fake message (SMS, email, call) that pretends to be from a bank or company to trick you into giving your details or clicking a link.

Warning signs of a scam message:

  • Urgent or scary words: "URGENT", "account locked", "act now".
  • A link asking you to "verify your details".
  • Requests for your PIN, password, card number or OTP (one-time code).
  • Unknown sender, spelling mistakes, offers that are too good to be true.
Sara's Saturday Payments: Sara receives a text: "URGENT: Your Gulf National Bank account has been locked. Tap here immediately to verify your details." Correct action: do NOT tap the link or reply. Contact the bank using its official app or the phone number on the back of the card, and tell a parent.
Safety rules: never share your PIN or OTP with anyone (a real bank never asks for them); cover the keypad; only pay on secure, trusted websites; turn on transaction alerts; report a lost card immediately.
⚠️ Banks do not ask you to "verify your details" by tapping a link in a text message.
