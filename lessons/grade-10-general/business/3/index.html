Managing Transactions (إدارة المعاملات)

💼 الدراسات التجارية🎓 الصف العاشر عام📌 هيكل الامتحان: M1.3 Financial Literacy

🎯 ذاكر الدرس وحل الكويز مجانًا في مذاكرتي

A transaction (معاملة مالية) is any movement of money in or out of your account: a purchase, a payment, a transfer or money received.

Bank statement (كشف الحساب): a list from the bank of all transactions in a period (e.g. one month), with the date, description, amount and balance.
Money in = credit (إيداع) e.g. pocket money arriving.
Money out = debit (خصم) e.g. supermarket, phone top-up, school trip.

Why track your money?

  • To know exactly how much you have and avoid overspending.
  • To spot mistakes, double charges or unknown charges.
  • To plan your saving goals.

Good habits: keep receipts, check your banking app regularly (e.g. every week), and compare receipts with your statement.

Rania's Bank Statement: In October she sees her supermarket visit, pocket money, a phone top-up and a school trip payment. One charge of AED 45 on 9 October says only "unknown charge". What should she do? Check her receipts and memory first, then contact her bank (and tell a parent) quickly so it can be investigated and the card blocked if needed.
⚠️ Never ignore an unknown charge – it may be a mistake or fraud. Report it to the bank as soon as possible.
