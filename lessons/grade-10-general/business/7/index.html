What is Marketing? (ما هو التسويق)

💼 الدراسات التجارية🎓 الصف العاشر عام📌 هيكل الامتحان: Unit 1 Introduction to Marketing

🎯 ذاكر الدرس وحل الكويز مجانًا في مذاكرتي
Marketing (التسويق) is the way a business communicates and connects with its target market to sell more products.

The word market (السوق) has several meanings in business:

  1. The place where a business sells its products.
  2. The people who want to buy a product.
  3. A particular area, country or section of the population that might buy a product.

Why is marketing important to businesses? Through marketing, businesses can:

  • find out what customers want;
  • find out information about customers;
  • predict market changes;
  • achieve customer loyalty (ولاء العملاء) – customers keep coming back.
A juice shop asks customers which new flavours they would like, learns that teenagers prefer smaller cheaper cups, and offers a loyalty card. This is marketing: understanding and connecting with customers.
⚠️ Marketing is not only advertising. It includes finding out what customers want, the product, its price and how it reaches customers.
