Marketing Mix: Product (المزيج التسويقي: المنتج)

💼 الدراسات التجارية🎓 الصف العاشر عام📌 هيكل الامتحان: Unit 3 Marketing Mix – Product

🎯 ذاكر الدرس وحل الكويز مجانًا في مذاكرتي
Marketing mix (المزيج التسويقي): all the activities that allow a business to market a product well. If a product is marketed well, many people will know about it and buy it. (The mix is often called the 4Ps: Product, Price, Place, Promotion.)

The product (المنتج) is the good or service that the business sells to customers. It is the most important part of the marketing mix, because without a product there is no business.

Characteristics of a good product:

  1. It should meet customers' needs or wants.
  2. It should have a special design to make it different from other products.
  3. Successful products are often first to the market.
  4. It can be changed to have new features.
  5. It should be possible to make a profit.

Product design (تصميم المنتج) has three parts:

Aesthetics (المظهر الجمالي)How the product looks
Function (الوظيفة)How the product works and what it does
Cost-effectiveness (الفعالية من حيث التكلفة)How easy it will be to make a profit
⚠️ True/False traps from class: "A product should look the same as other products" – FALSE. "Successful products are often last to market" – FALSE. "A business should not add new features" – FALSE.
