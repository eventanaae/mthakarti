Market Segmentation & Target Markets (تجزئة السوق والسوق المستهدف)

💼 الدراسات التجارية🎓 الصف العاشر عام📌 هيكل الامتحان: Unit 1 Introduction to Marketing

🎯 ذاكر الدرس وحل الكويز مجانًا في مذاكرتي
Market segmentation (تجزئة السوق): dividing a market into smaller groups of customers with similar features.
Target market (السوق المستهدف): the group of customers a business aims to sell its product to.
Geographic segmentation (جغرافي) – WHERE customers liveDemographic segmentation (ديموغرافي) – WHO customers are

A company selling warm winter jackets targets countries with a cold climate → geographic.
A company selling a cheaper phone for students on low incomes → demographic (age and income).

Why segment? The business can design the right product, set the right price and advertise in the right place for each group, so it wastes less money and sells more.

⚠️ "Climate" and "population density" are geographic, not demographic. "Interests" and "jobs" are demographic.

✅ الجواب: Geographic
