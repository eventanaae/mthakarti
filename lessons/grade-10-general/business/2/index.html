Payment Methods (طرق الدفع)

💼 الدراسات التجارية🎓 الصف العاشر عام📌 هيكل الامتحان: M1.2 Financial Literacy

🎯 ذاكر الدرس وحل الكويز مجانًا في مذاكرتي

A payment method (طريقة الدفع) is the way you pay for something. Each method has advantages and limits.

MethodHow it works
Cash (نقد)Paying with notes and coins. Works everywhere, even with no card reader.
Debit card (بطاقة خصم)Takes money directly from your bank account. You can only spend what you have.
Credit card (بطاقة ائتمان)You borrow money from the bank and pay it back later (interest may be charged if late).
Mobile / contactless payment (الدفع عبر الهاتف)Tap a card or phone on the terminal.
Online payment / bank transfer (الدفع الإلكتروني / التحويل)Paying on websites and apps, or sending money between accounts.
Omar's Weekend: Omar has a debit card (balance AED 320) and AED 60 cash.
• Groceries at LuLu AED 95 – card terminal available → debit card.
• Pay a classmate back AED 40 – no card reader → cash.
• Book on Noon.com AED 55 – online → debit card.
Cash left: 60 − 40 = AED 20. Card balance left: 320 − 95 − 55 = AED 170.
⚠️ Before using a new payment method, check: is it safe and trusted? Are there fees? Do I have enough money?

Which payment method takes money directly from your own bank account?

When you pay with a credit card, you are…

✅ الجواب: Borrowing money that you must pay back later

A credit card is borrowed money that must be repaid.

Omar has AED 60 cash and pays his classmate back AED 40 in cash. How much cash is left?

✅ الجواب: AED 20

60 - 40 = 20 dirhams of cash left.
