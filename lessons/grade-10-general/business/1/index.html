Forms and Purposes of Money (أشكال النقود ووظائفها)

💼 الدراسات التجارية🎓 الصف العاشر عام📌 هيكل الامتحان: M1.1 Financial Literacy

🎯 ذاكر الدرس وحل الكويز مجانًا في مذاكرتي

Money (النقود) is anything people accept in exchange for goods and services. Today we use money in two main forms:

FormExamplesGood for
Cash (النقد) – physical moneyBanknotes and coins (dirhams and fils)Small shops or kiosks with no card reader, paying a friend back
Digital money (النقود الرقمية)Money in a bank account, debit card, mobile walletSupermarkets, online shopping, keeping savings safe
Purposes (functions) of money:
  • Medium of exchange (وسيلة للتبادل) – we use it to buy and sell.
  • Store of value (مخزن للقيمة) – we can save it and use it later.
  • Unit of account (مقياس للقيمة) – it lets us compare prices.
Real-World Moment – Tariq's Savings: Tariq gets AED 200 as an Eid gift. He keeps AED 80 in his wallet and deposits the rest (AED 120) in his bank account. He is saving for football boots costing AED 350. He wants a snack from a kiosk with no card reader, so he must use his cash.

Money in a bank account is safer and easier to save, but cash is sometimes the only option. Many people find cash easier to control, because they can see it leaving their wallet.

⚠️ Digital money is still real money! Spending with a card feels "easier", so it is easy to overspend.

✅ الجواب: Cash

With no card reader, only cash works.
