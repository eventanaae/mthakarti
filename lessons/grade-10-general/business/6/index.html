Types and Sources of Income (أنواع الدخل ومصادره)

💼 الدراسات التجارية🎓 الصف العاشر عام📌 هيكل الامتحان: M1.6 Financial Literacy

🎯 ذاكر الدرس وحل الكويز مجانًا في مذاكرتي

Income (الدخل) is money you receive. It can be regular (the same amount at the same time) or variable (it changes).

SourceMeaningReliable?
Allowance / pocket money (مصروف)Regular amount from familyFixed – easy to plan
Salary (راتب)Fixed amount paid every month for a jobFixed – easy to plan
Wages (أجر)Paid per hour workedVariable if hours change
Commission (عمولة)Extra money for each item soldVariable – depends on sales
Gifts (هدايا)e.g. Eid moneyIrregular – cannot plan around it
Aisha, Hamdan and Fatima: Aisha gets a regular allowance of AED 200 a month. Hamdan earns AED 25 per hour at a shop but his hours vary. Fatima earns a small base amount plus AED 15 commission per phone accessory sold. Most reliable income to plan with: Aisha (fixed and regular).
Example: if Hamdan works 6 hours, he earns 6 × 25 = AED 150; if Fatima sells 4 accessories, her commission is 4 × 15 = AED 60 (plus her base).
⚠️ Variable income makes planning harder – plan your spending using the lowest amount you expect to earn.

اختبر نفسك: أسئلة على Types and Sources of Income (أنواع الدخل ومصادره)

Aisha gets AED 200 every month, Hamdan earns AED 25/hour with changing hours, Fatima earns a small base plus commission. Who has the most reliable income?

✅ الجواب: Aisha

A fixed, regular allowance is the easiest income to plan with.

Hamdan earns AED 25 per hour. How much does he earn for 6 hours?

✅ الجواب: AED 150

6 x 25 = 150 dirhams.

Fatima earns AED 15 commission per accessory. What does commission depend on?

✅ الجواب: How many items she sells

Commission is paid per item sold, so it depends on sales.
