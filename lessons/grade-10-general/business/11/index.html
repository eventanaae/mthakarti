Primary and Secondary Research (البحث الأولي والثانوي)

💼 الدراسات التجارية🎓 الصف العاشر عام📌 هيكل الامتحان: Unit 2 Market Research

🎯 ذاكر الدرس وحل الكويز مجانًا في مذاكرتي
Primary research (البحث الأولي / الميداني)Secondary research (البحث الثانوي / المكتبي)
Research carried out by the business itself – new informationUsing information that has already been gathered by someone else
• Questionnaires (استبيانات)
• Interviews (مقابلات)
• Focus groups (مجموعات التركيز)
• Observations (الملاحظة)
• Test market (السوق التجريبي)		• Internet
• Government reports
• Books
• Industry reports
• Business records
• Newspapers and magazines
✔ Up to date and specific to the business's questions
✘ Takes time and costs more		✔ Quick and cheap (often free)
✘ May be out of date or not exactly what the business needs
Focus group: a small group of customers discuss a product and give their opinions.
Test market: selling a new product in one small area first to see if people buy it.
Observation: watching how customers behave (e.g. counting people entering a shop).
⚠️ Business records are secondary research (they already exist), even though they belong to the business.

اختبر نفسك: أسئلة على Primary and Secondary Research (البحث الأولي والثانوي)

Primary research is…

✅ الجواب: Research carried out by the business itself

Primary research is new research done by the business itself.

Which is a method of primary research?

✅ الجواب: Questionnaire

Questionnaires collect new data, so they are primary research.

Which is a source of secondary research?

✅ الجواب: Business records

Business records already exist, so they are secondary.
