Value for Money (القيمة مقابل المال)

💼 الدراسات التجارية🎓 الصف العاشر عام📌 هيكل الامتحان: M1.4 Financial Literacy

🎯 ذاكر الدرس وحل الكويز مجانًا في مذاكرتي

Value for money (القيمة مقابل المال) means getting the best benefit for the money you spend – not always the cheapest price.

Needs (الاحتياجات) – things you must have (food, school supplies, transport).
Wants (الرغبات) – things that are nice to have but not essential (a new game, designer trainers).
Whether something is a need or a want can depend on the situation.

How to judge value for money:

  • Compare prices in different shops and online.
  • Compare unit price – the price per item, per page or per gram.
  • Quality and how long it lasts – a cheap item that breaks quickly is poor value.
  • Guarantee / warranty (الضمان) – protection if the product is faulty.
Trade-off (المفاضلة): when you choose one thing, you give up something else. Every choice with a limited budget is a trade-off.
Khalid's School Supplies: Khalid has AED 100 but must keep AED 10 for the bus home, so he can spend only AED 90 on notebooks and a school bag. A stronger bag with a guarantee may cost more but last longer – he must balance price, quality and his budget.
⚠️ Cheapest ≠ best value. Look at quality, size and guarantee too.
