Market Research & Types of Data (بحوث السوق وأنواع البيانات)

💼 الدراسات التجارية🎓 الصف العاشر عام📌 هيكل الامتحان: Unit 2 Market Research

🎯 ذاكر الدرس وحل الكويز مجانًا في مذاكرتي
Market research (بحوث السوق) is collecting information about customers, competition and the market.

Market research helps a business to:

  • develop new products;
  • find out what customers want;
  • keep customers satisfied;
  • check up on competition;
  • find market gaps (فجوة في السوق) – a need no business is meeting yet;
  • predict changes.
Quantitative data (بيانات كمية)Qualitative data (بيانات نوعية)
Related to numbersRelated to people's feelings or opinions
"120 students bought juice this week"; "65% prefer mango""I like the taste because it is not too sweet"

Businesses can display research results in different ways, e.g. tables, bar charts, pie charts and line graphs, so they are easy to understand.

⚠️ A percentage or a count is quantitative even if it is about opinions (e.g. "70% said they like it"). A written reason or comment is qualitative.
