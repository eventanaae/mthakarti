Pricing Methods & PED (طرق التسعير ومرونة الطلب السعرية)

🎯 ذاكر الدرس وحل الكويز مجانًا في مذاكرتي
MethodUsed byBenefitDrawback
Cost-plus pricing (التسعير بالتكلفة مضافًا إليها هامش): cost + extra amount for profitA business that only produces one productGuaranteed profit on each productPrice may be too high
Competitive pricing (التسعير التنافسي): price similar to competitorsA business that has many competitorsCan sell its products and keep customersMay be difficult to make high profits
Price skimming (القشط السعري): high price at launch, lowered laterA business that sells innovative technological productsHelps cover high development costsSome people may not be able to afford it
Promotional pricing (التسعير الترويجي): low price for a short timeA business that wants to increase sales or enter a new marketHelps customers recognise a product and brandMay make the product look cheap
Premium pricing (التسعير المرتفع / الفاخر): high price to show exclusivityA business that sells luxury items and wants the product to be exclusiveCan maximise profitsHigh advertising costs
Price elasticity of demand (PED) (مرونة الطلب السعرية) shows how much the demand for a product changes if the business changes the price.
The demand curve slopes down: when price falls from P1 to P2, quantity demanded rises from Q1 to Q2.
Price elastic: demand changes a lot when price changes. Price inelastic: demand changes only a little.
⚠️ Skimming = HIGH price first (new technology). Promotional = LOW price for a short time. Don't mix them up!
