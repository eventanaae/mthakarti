How Markets Change & Competition (تغيّر الأسواق والمنافسة)

💼 الدراسات التجارية🎓 الصف العاشر عام📌 هيكل الامتحان: Unit 1 Introduction to Marketing

🎯 ذاكر الدرس وحل الكويز مجانًا في مذاكرتي

Markets do not stay the same. Products that were popular before may disappear and new ones replace them (for example, how people listen to music, take photos or make phone calls has changed a lot).

Why do markets change?

  • New technology (التكنولوجيا الحديثة) – e.g. smartphones replaced many separate devices.
  • Changing customer tastes and lifestyles – e.g. more people want healthy food or shop online.
  • New competitors entering the market.

Businesses that do not change with the market may lose customers.

Competition (المنافسة) is when businesses are selling similar products to the same target market.

Competition is when businesses sell…

✅ الجواب: Similar products to the same target market

Competitors sell similar products to the same target market.

Which pair are competitors?

✅ الجواب: Pepsi and Coca-Cola

Pepsi and Coca-Cola sell similar drinks to the same customers.

Why are H&M and Louis Vuitton not really competitors?

✅ الجواب: They target very different markets (budget vs luxury)

Budget and luxury brands target very different customers.
