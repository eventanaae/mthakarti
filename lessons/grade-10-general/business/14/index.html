Marketing Mix: Price & Factors Influencing Price (السعر والعوامل المؤثرة فيه)

💼 الدراسات التجارية🎓 الصف العاشر عام📌 هيكل الامتحان: Unit 4 Marketing Mix – Price

🎯 ذاكر الدرس وحل الكويز مجانًا في مذاكرتي
Price (السعر) is how much a customer must pay for a product.

Why is price important?

  • It earns revenue (الإيرادات) for the business.
  • It sends a message about quality: a high price suggests high quality; a low price may suggest lower quality.
  • If the price is too high, customers might not be able to afford it.

Branded vs own-brand products: branded products (e.g. a famous brand of cereal) usually cost more; own-brand products (منتجات العلامة الخاصة بالمتجر) are made for and sold under the supermarket's own name and are usually cheaper.

Factors influencing price:

Costs (التكاليف)The product must be sold for more than it costs to make, so the business can make a profit.
Price of similar productsThe business decides whether to charge higher or lower prices than competitors, based on design and quality.
Demand (الطلب)Businesses must charge prices they know customers will pay.
Laws (القوانين)Many governments set laws to make sure businesses charge fair prices.
⚠️ Price earns revenue; profit is what is left after costs are paid.
