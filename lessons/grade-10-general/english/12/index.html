Reading: Climate Context Clues & Clouds on Command

The idea in detail

When you meet a new word in a text, you do not need to open a dictionary immediately. Good readers use context clues (القرائن السياقية): the words and sentences around the new word. In the exam you cannot use a dictionary, so this is a very important skill (the "inference" question asks for the meaning of a word from context).

Steps to guess a meaning

  1. Decide the part of speech (noun, verb, adjective?).
  2. Look for a signal word or a clue in the same sentence or the next one.
  3. Guess the meaning.
  4. Replace the word with your guess and check that the sentence makes sense.

You can also use word parts: prefixes (un-, dis-, re-) and suffixes (-able, -ful, -less). Example: unpredictable = un (not) + predict + able, so "not possible to predict".

Clouds on Command: cloud seeding

The UAE is a dry country with little rain, so it uses cloud seeding (الاستمطار) to increase rainfall. Planes fly into suitable clouds and release salt particles. Tiny water droplets collect around these particles, become heavier and fall as rain. This gives the country more fresh water. Note that seeding needs clouds to exist; it cannot create rain from a clear sky.

Example 1. "The region is known for its scorching summers, when temperatures can reach 50 degrees." What does scorching mean? Clue: "temperatures can reach 50 degrees" (general sense/example). Meaning: extremely hot.
Example 2 (contrast). "Unlike the humid coast, the inland desert is arid." The signal word unlike shows an opposite. Humid = full of water vapour, so arid = very dry.
⚠️ (1) With contrast clues, the meaning is the opposite; do not choose the same meaning. (2) Do not choose an option just because it is a common meaning; the word may have another meaning in this text (for example "bank"). (3) Check the part of speech: a noun cannot be replaced by an adjective. (4) Cloud seeding does not make clouds; it helps existing clouds produce rain.
Exam corner. In Part 3A there is 1 inference question (word meaning from context) and in Part 3B also 1. Write the sentence with each option in the place of the word and choose the one that keeps the meaning. Often the answer is found in the sentence before or after. Also relevant for Part 1 vocabulary: humidity, climate, season, absorb, reflect.

Quick summary

  • Context clues: definition, synonym, antonym, example, general sense.
  • Look for signal words (which is, or, but, such as).
  • Check your guess in the sentence.
  • Cloud seeding: salt particles in clouds make rain; it helps a dry country.
