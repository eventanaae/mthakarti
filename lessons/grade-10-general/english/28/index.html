Writing 1.2: Argumentative Text (150 words)

🌦️ اللغة الإنجليزية🎓 الصف العاشر عام📌 هيكل الامتحان: Part 4 Writing 1.2 — 'Write about whether you think…' with 3 prompts (25 marks)

🎯 ذاكر الدرس وحل الكويز مجانًا في مذاكرتي

Task 1.2: "Write about whether you think ______. Include: prompt 1, prompt 2, prompt 3. Write at least 150 words in paragraphs." Themes: Talking About Seasons or Ready for a Shared-Living Experience? (e.g. whether summer is the best season; whether sharing a room with a roommate is a good idea).

Structure (4 short paragraphs)
1. Introduction – restate the question + say your opinion (thesis).
2. Body 1 – topic sentence + reason + explanation/example (prompt 1 and 2).
3. Body 2 – another reason (prompt 3) or the opposite view + your reply.
4. Conclusion – restate your opinion in new words; no new ideas.

Language: Opinion – In my opinion, I strongly believe that, From my point of view, I agree/disagree that. Reasons – because, since, This is why, For example/instance. Contrast – However, On the other hand, Although. Result – Therefore, As a result. Necessity – students have to / must / need to…, should… Conclude – In conclusion, To sum up.

Model (prompts: privacy, cost, friendship):
I believe that sharing a room with a roommate is a good idea for university students.
Firstly, it saves money. Rent is expensive, so two students who share a room pay half the price. As a result, they can spend more on books and food, which is very important for students who do not have a large budget.
Secondly, a roommate can become a friend. For example, my cousin met her best friend in the dormitory, and they now study together every evening. Living with someone also helps students feel less lonely when they are away from home. However, privacy can be a problem. Students must agree on simple rules, such as quiet hours, so that everyone can sleep and study. They should also talk honestly if something bothers them.
In conclusion, although privacy needs careful planning, the benefits of lower cost and new friendships make sharing a room a smart choice for most university students.

Checklist: ✔ all three prompts covered ✔ clear opinion ✔ paragraphs ✔ linkers ✔ at least 150 words ✔ check spelling, verb tenses, punctuation.

⚠️ One long paragraph, no opinion, ignoring a prompt, or fewer than 150 words loses marks. Keep to the topic and do not write a story.

The idea in detail

Task 1.2 is worth 25 marks. The instruction is: "Write about whether you think ______. Include: prompt 1, prompt 2, prompt 3. Write at least 150 words in paragraphs." The themes are Talking About Seasons and Ready for a Shared-Living Experience? (for example: whether summer is the best season, or whether sharing a room is a good idea).

An argumentative text (نص جدلي) gives your opinion and supports it with reasons and examples. You are like a lawyer: your opinion is the claim and the reasons are the proof. You must cover all three prompts, because each prompt is a mark point.

Structure: 4 short paragraphs

  1. Introduction: restate the question and give your opinion (thesis).
  2. Body 1: topic sentence + reason + explanation or example (covering prompts 1 and 2).
  3. Body 2: another reason (prompt 3) or the opposite view with your reply.
  4. Conclusion: restate your opinion in new words; do not add new ideas.

Language bank. Opinion: In my opinion, I strongly believe that, From my point of view, I agree that. Reasons: because, since, This is why, For example. Contrast: However, On the other hand, Although. Result: Therefore, As a result. Obligation: students have to, must, need to, should. Conclusion: In conclusion, To sum up.

Example 1 (model answer, prompts: cost, friendship, privacy).
I believe that sharing a room with a roommate is a good idea for university students.

Firstly, it saves money. Rent is expensive, so two students who share a room pay half the price. As a result, they can spend more on books and food, which is important for students who do not have a large budget.

Secondly, a roommate can become a friend. For example, my cousin met her best friend in the dormitory and they now study together every evening. Living with someone also helps students feel less lonely when they are away from home. However, privacy can be a problem. Students must agree on simple rules, such as quiet hours, so that everyone can sleep and study.

In conclusion, although privacy needs careful planning, the benefits of lower cost and new friendships make sharing a room a smart choice for most university students.
(About 150 words, so add one more sentence of explanation to be safe; three prompts covered; linkers: Firstly, As a result, For example, However, In conclusion.)
Example 2 (seasons: opening and a body paragraph). Question: whether winter is the best season in the UAE. Introduction: "I strongly believe that winter is the best season in the UAE because of the weather and the activities." Body: "First, the temperature is mild, between 14 and 25 degrees, so people can spend time outdoors without suffering from the heat. Moreover, families can enjoy camping and picnics in the desert. This is why many tourists visit the UAE in winter."

Checklist: all three prompts covered; clear opinion; paragraphs; linkers; at least 150 words; check spelling, verb tenses and punctuation.

⚠️ (1) Writing one long paragraph. (2) Not giving an opinion, or changing it. (3) Ignoring a prompt. (4) Writing fewer than 150 words. (5) Writing a story instead of an argument. (6) Giving a reason without explanation ("It is cheap" is not enough; add why this matters). (7) Using informal words like "kids", "stuff", "gonna".
Exam corner. The task is marked for content (all prompts), organisation (paragraphs and linkers), vocabulary and grammar accuracy. For full marks: opinion in the introduction, one reason per prompt with an example, one contrast sentence, a conclusion, and a final check of verb forms. A 150-word text means about 12 to 15 sentences.

Quick summary

  • 25 marks; at least 150 words; paragraphs; all 3 prompts.
  • Introduction (opinion), two body paragraphs (reasons + examples), conclusion.
  • Use opinion phrases, linkers and modals of obligation.
  • Check spelling and tenses at the end.

اختبر نفسك: أسئلة على Writing 1.2: Argumentative Text (150 words)

Task: "Write about whether you think summer is the best season." Which is the best opening sentence?

✅ الجواب: In my opinion, summer is not the best season because of the extreme heat in the UAE.

A strong introduction states your opinion clearly.

A body paragraph in an argumentative text should contain…

✅ الجواب: a topic sentence, a reason and an explanation or example

Body paragraphs develop one reason with support.

Which is the best CONCLUSION for an essay about sharing a room?

✅ الجواب: To sum up, I believe sharing a room is a good idea if roommates respect each other's privacy.

A conclusion restates your opinion without adding new ideas.
