The idea in detail

Part 1 of the exam is a vocabulary gap-fill. The text has 5 gaps and each gap has 3 options. To choose correctly you need two things: the meaning of the word and its grammar (part of speech and the words that go with it). This lesson explains ten important words from the themes "Meeting New People" and "Learning New Things".

WordMeaningWord family / pattern
roommatea person who shares your room or flatroom + mate; classmate, teammate
colleaguea person you work withnoun; a colleague at work
privacybeing free from other people's attentionprivate (adj.), privately (adv.)
communitya group of people in one area or with shared interestsa school community, community centre
deadlinethe last date or time to finish somethingmeet a deadline, miss a deadline
schedulea plan of times and tasksfollow a schedule, on schedule
authoritythe power to decide; an official group in chargethe authorities, local authority
regulationan official rulefollow the regulations; regulate (verb)
manageto organise or control; to succeed in doing something hardmanage time; manage to + verb
cope withto deal with a difficult situationcope with stress

How to choose the right word

  1. Skim the whole text for the topic (it helps with the meaning).
  2. Read the sentence with the gap and decide the part of speech: noun, verb or adjective?
  3. Look at the words around the gap: a/the before = noun; to before = verb; "with" after = cope.
  4. Put each option in the gap and read it. Choose the one that is logical and grammatical.
Example 1. "Many first-year students find it hard to ____ with homesickness." Options: A) manage B) cope C) schedule. The word "with" after the gap fits cope (cope with). "Manage" would need an object or "to" (manage to do). Answer: B.
Example 2. "The housing ____ has a new rule: no loud music after ten o'clock." Options: A) authority B) privacy C) deadline. A rule is created by someone in charge: authority. Privacy and deadline do not make rules. Answer: A.
Another: "Please hand in your essay before the ____ on Friday." Answer: deadline (the last date).
⚠️ (1) colleague is a person at work, classmate at school, roommate in your room. (2) regulation = the rule; authority = who makes or enforces the rule. (3) manage + to-infinitive (manage to finish), but cope + with. (4) Do not choose a word because you recognise it; check the grammar. (5) privacy is a noun; the adjective is private.
Exam corner. Part 1 = 5 gaps x 2 marks = 10 marks. The distractors are often words with similar meaning but different grammar. Read the full sentence, find the clue words (with, to, a, the) and eliminate. These words are also useful in your writing about shared living.

Quick summary

  • roommate (shares room), colleague (work), community (group), privacy (alone).
  • deadline = last date; schedule = plan; authority = power; regulation = rule.
  • manage to + verb; cope with + noun.
  • Gap-fill: meaning + grammar + clue words.
