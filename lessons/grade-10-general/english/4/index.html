Writing: Sharing Economy Opinions

🌦️ اللغة الإنجليزية🎓 الصف العاشر عام📌 هيكل الامتحان: Ch1 Writing — advantages/disadvantages & opinion

🎯 ذاكر الدرس وحل الكويز مجانًا في مذاكرتي

The sharing economy is a system where people share resources such as cars, bikes, homes, tools or workspaces, often through apps (e.g. ride-sharing apps, bike-sharing, peer-to-peer services).

Model essay structure (dockless bike-sharing text): Para A = introduction/thesis · Paras B & C = advantages · Paras D & E = disadvantages · Para F = conclusion.

Useful language
Opinion: I believe that…, In my opinion,…, It could be argued that…, It seems that…, This suggests that…
Advantages: One benefit/merit is…, Furthermore, In addition
Disadvantages: One drawback/demerit is…, However,…
Examples/reasons: For example, because, In some cases
Contrast: On the other hand, Although
Conclusion: In conclusion, Overall, For the reasons named above
"The sharing economy has many benefits, such as saving money. However, it also has disadvantages, including lack of regulation and job insecurity. In my opinion, its advantages are greater."
⚠️ Although joins two clauses in ONE sentence (Although it is cheap, it is not reliable). However starts a new sentence and takes a comma.

The idea in detail

The sharing economy (الاقتصاد التشاركي) is a way of using things where people share or rent items instead of each person buying their own. Examples: ride-sharing apps, bike-sharing, renting a room for a holiday, sharing tools or a working space. Apps connect the owner and the user. The idea is simple: a car or a bike is unused most of the day, so another person can use it and pay.

This topic is used to practise opinion writing: you weigh the advantages (benefits) and the disadvantages (drawbacks) and then give your own opinion with reasons.

Organising an opinion essay

A model essay on dockless bike-sharing has six paragraphs: A = introduction with your thesis (opinion), B and C = advantages, D and E = disadvantages, F = conclusion. In a short exam answer you can use only four paragraphs: introduction, advantage, disadvantage, conclusion. Remember that a good writer always explains each point with a reason or example.

Useful language. Opinion: I believe that, In my opinion, It could be argued that. Benefits: One benefit is, Furthermore, In addition. Drawbacks: One drawback is, However. Contrast: On the other hand, Although. Conclusion: In conclusion, Overall.

Example 1 (a body paragraph). "One major benefit of bike-sharing is that it saves money. Students do not have to buy an expensive bike; they only pay a small fee for each ride. As a result, they can spend more on food and books. Furthermore, fewer cars on the road means cleaner air."
Example 2 (balanced opinion). "Although the sharing economy has some drawbacks, such as a lack of regulation, I believe that its advantages are greater. It helps people save money and protects the environment. However, governments should make clear safety rules so that users and workers are protected."
Notice: the sentence starts with Although (one sentence, one comma) and the next one uses However (new sentence).
⚠️ (1) "Although ... but ..." is wrong: use only one of them. (2) However is not a conjunction: "It is cheap, however it is slow" is a comma splice. Write "It is cheap. However, it is slow." (3) Do not give only advantages when the task asks for both sides. (4) Do not write an opinion without a reason.
Exam corner. The Writing 1.2 task is "Write about whether you think...". Even if the topic is different (seasons or shared living), the method is the same: opinion, two or three reasons with examples, a contrast with However or Although, and a conclusion. Keep at least 150 words and cover every prompt in the question.

Quick summary

  • Sharing economy = sharing or renting things, usually through apps.
  • Advantages: save money, less waste and pollution, community. Disadvantages: unreliable, low regulation, job insecurity.
  • Structure: introduction, advantages, disadvantages, conclusion.
  • Although = same sentence; However = new sentence plus comma.
