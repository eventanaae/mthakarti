Speaking: A Nature Story, Rules & Seasons

🌦️ اللغة الإنجليزية🎓 الصف العاشر عام📌 هيكل الامتحان: L&S Ch2 Speaking 1–3

🎯 ذاكر الدرس وحل الكويز مجانًا في مذاكرتي

Speaking 1 – Telling a nature story from pictures. Describe pictures with the present continuous and present simple, then retell it in the past.

Chloe and Ryan are sitting in a jeep. They look excited. They start hiking up the mountain; the weather is clear and they enjoy the view. They are climbing up some rocks carefully. The ground is muddy and Ryan steps on a slippery log. There is a warning sign for falling rocks, but they don't see it. In the end, Ryan is at home with his leg in a cast – he broke it during the hike.

Storytelling tips: use sequence words (first, then, suddenly, in the end), describe feelings, and use your voice (pause before the surprise).

Speaking 2 – Explaining rules (park signs: No open fires, Use trash cans, No drones, Slow down for wildlife, Please shut gate).

Modals for rules
must / need to / have to = necessary: You must use the trash cans.
mustn't = not allowed: You mustn't light open fires. (= it is illegal)
should = advice: You should slow down for wildlife.
shouldn't = bad idea: You shouldn't feed the animals.
don't have to = not necessary (but allowed).

Speaking 3 – Talking about seasons (a prepared presentation). Class data for the UAE: Spring Mar–May 25–38°C, dry and sunny · Summer Jun–Aug 35–45°C, extremely hot, dusty · Fall Sep–Nov 25–38°C, warm, less humid · Winter Dec–Feb 14–25°C, mild, cooler evenings.

⚠️ mustn't ≠ don't have to. "You mustn't camp here" = it's forbidden. "You don't have to pay" = it's free, payment isn't necessary.

The idea in detail

This lesson has three speaking tasks: telling a nature story from pictures, explaining park rules, and talking about the seasons.

1. Telling a nature story

Look at the pictures in order. Describe what is happening in each picture with the present continuous (They are climbing) and present simple (The weather is clear). If you retell it afterwards, use the past tenses. A good story has: who, where, what happened, a problem and an ending. Use sequence words (First, Then, Suddenly, In the end), describe feelings (excited, worried) and use your voice (pause before the surprise).

In the lesson story, Chloe and Ryan hike up a mountain. The ground is muddy and Ryan steps on a slippery log. There is a sign about falling rocks that they do not see. In the end, Ryan is at home with his leg in a cast because he broke it.

2. Explaining rules (park signs)

Signs: No open fires, Use trash cans, No drones, Slow down for wildlife, Please shut the gate.

3. Talking about seasons

UAE data from the class: Spring (Mar to May): 25 to 38 degrees, dry and sunny. Summer (Jun to Aug): 35 to 45 degrees, extremely hot and dusty. Fall (Sep to Nov): 25 to 38 degrees, warm and less humid. Winter (Dec to Feb): 14 to 25 degrees, mild with cooler evenings. A good presentation says the season, months, temperature, weather, and activities, then a personal opinion.

Example 1 (explaining a sign). Sign "No drones". "You mustn't fly drones here because they can frighten the animals and disturb other visitors. You should check the park rules before you come."
Example 2 (mini presentation). "My favourite season is winter. It lasts from December to February. The temperature is between 14 and 25 degrees, so it is mild and the evenings are cooler. In winter my family goes camping in the desert and we enjoy the fresh air. I prefer winter to summer because I can spend time outdoors."
⚠️ (1) mustn't (forbidden) is different from don't have to (not necessary). (2) After modals use the base verb: "must use", not "must to use". (3) Use the present continuous for actions happening now in the picture, not "climb" alone. (4) Do not read a long text; speak using notes and look at your audience. (5) "Fall" and "autumn" are the same season.
Exam corner. The functions "expressing necessity and obligation" (FL.20) and "describing past experiences" appear in Writing 1.2 under the themes "Talking About Seasons" and "Shared Living". Also "modals of ability" and "adverbial linkers" are tested in Part 2. Use the correct modal and give reasons.

Quick summary

  • Tell a story with pictures: present continuous, sequence words, feelings.
  • must/have to = necessary; mustn't = forbidden; should = advice; don't have to = not necessary.
  • Seasons of the UAE: spring, summer, fall, winter with temperature ranges.
  • Present with notes, give an opinion and reasons.
