Writing 1.1: Summarising the Main Ideas in Your Own Words

🌦️ اللغة الإنجليزية🎓 الصف العاشر عام📌 هيكل الامتحان: Part 4 Writing 1.1 — ENG.06.WR.S.6.1 (15 marks, 3–4 sentences)

🎯 ذاكر الدرس وحل الكويز مجانًا في مذاكرتي

Task 1.1: Read the text (about 170 words) and write the main ideas in your own words in 3–4 sentences. The text is related to the theme (seasons or shared living).

Steps
1. Read the text once for the general idea, then again; underline the main idea of each paragraph.
2. Cross out examples, repeated points and small details.
3. Write 3–4 sentences, in the same order as the text, from memory (do not copy).
4. Use synonyms and change the sentence structure. Do not add your opinion.
Paraphrasing techniqueOriginalParaphrase
SynonymsThe weather was extremely hot.The temperature was very high.
Change word formRoommates should respect each other's privacy.Respect for privacy is important between roommates.
Change structureBecause it was raining, we stayed in.We stayed inside due to the rain.
Join / shortenHe opened the door. He walked in.He opened the door and walked in.
Text: "Many students move to a new city to study. At first they often feel lonely and find it hard to manage their time. However, joining clubs and making a weekly schedule helps them make friends and stay organised. Universities also offer advice centres."
Summary: Students who move away to study can feel lonely and disorganised. Clubs and schedules help them make friends and manage time, and universities also provide advice centres.
⚠️ Common mistakes: copying whole sentences; adding "I think…"; including a small example as if it were the main idea; writing more than 4 sentences or only one.

The idea in detail

Task 1.1 is worth 15 marks. You read a text of about 170 words (related to seasons or shared living) and write its main ideas in your own words in 3 to 4 sentences. This is a summary (ملخص). A summary is short, includes only the most important information, keeps the original order and does not include your opinion.

The steps

  1. Read once for the general idea and again to underline the main idea of each paragraph.
  2. Cross out examples, repeated points and small details.
  3. Close the text or look away and write the ideas from memory (so you do not copy).
  4. Write 3 to 4 sentences in the same order as the text.
  5. Check: ideas correct? own words? 3 to 4 sentences? grammar and spelling?

How to paraphrase (إعادة الصياغة)

TechniqueOriginalParaphrase
SynonymsThe weather was extremely hot.The temperature was very high.
Change word formRoommates should respect each other's privacy.Respect for privacy is important between roommates.
Change structureBecause it was raining, we stayed in.We stayed inside due to the rain.
Join sentencesHe opened the door. He walked in.He opened the door and walked in.
Example 1. Text: "Many students move to a new city to study. At first they often feel lonely and find it hard to manage their time. However, joining clubs and making a weekly schedule helps them make friends and stay organised. Universities also offer advice centres."
Main ideas: (1) students feel lonely and disorganised; (2) clubs and schedules help; (3) universities give advice.
Summary: Students who move away to study can feel lonely and disorganised. Joining clubs and planning a weekly schedule helps them make friends and manage their time, and universities also provide advice centres.
Example 2. Text: "In summer the UAE is extremely hot, so many people stay indoors during the day. They go out in the evening when the temperature is lower. For example, families often visit parks and beaches after sunset. In winter the weather is mild and people enjoy outdoor activities."
Main ideas: summer is hot, so people stay indoors and go out in the evening; winter is mild and good for outdoor activities. (The parks and beaches sentence is an example, so leave it out.)
Summary: Because summers in the UAE are very hot, people stay inside by day and go out when it gets cooler in the evening. In winter the mild weather lets them enjoy outdoor activities.
⚠️ (1) Copying whole sentences from the text. (2) Adding "I think" or your own opinion. (3) Including an example as if it were a main idea. (4) Writing more than 4 sentences or only one. (5) Changing the meaning when paraphrasing: do not change "mild" to "cold". (6) Changing the order of ideas.
Exam corner. Task 1.1 (15 marks): the marks are for including the main ideas, using your own words, accuracy and clear sentences. Model approach: one sentence per main idea, joined with linkers such as "and", "also", "as a result". Use words like "the text explains that" at most once. Aim for exactly 3 or 4 sentences.

Quick summary

  • Summary = main ideas only, in your own words, in order, no opinion.
  • 3 to 4 sentences; 15 marks.
  • Paraphrase: synonyms, word form, structure, joining.
  • Cross out examples and details.
