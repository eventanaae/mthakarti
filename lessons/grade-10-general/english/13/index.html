Writing: Weather Connections & Comparing Weather

🌦️ اللغة الإنجليزية🎓 الصف العاشر عام📌 هيكل الامتحان: Ch2 Writing — cause/effect & comparison

🎯 ذاكر الدرس وحل الكويز مجانًا في مذاكرتي

1) Cause and effect (from the class answer key)

The thunderstorm struck, so there was no power.
Mary heard about the hurricane warning, therefore she looked for a safe place.
Since the weather was bad, she had to stay inside. / She had to stay inside due to the bad weather.
Cause words: because, since, as + clause · because of / due to + noun
Effect words: so + clause · therefore / as a result / consequently (new clause, often after ; or .)

2) Comparing weather – a comparison paragraph shows similarities and differences between two places or seasons.

  • Similarity: both… , similarly, likewise, also, as … as
  • Difference: however, whereas, while, but, on the other hand, unlike, comparatives
Model: Dubai and Al Ain both have a hot desert climate. Both cities are extremely hot in summer. However, Dubai is on the coast, so it is more humid, whereas Al Ain is inland and drier. Similarly, both enjoy mild winters, but nights in Al Ain are cooler. Overall, the two cities are similar in temperature but different in humidity.

Organise it point by point (temperature → humidity → rain) or block (all about place A, then place B).

⚠️ because + clause (because it rained) but because of + noun (because of the rain). "Due to it rained" ✗.

The idea in detail

This lesson gives you two writing tools: cause and effect and comparison. Both are used to explain weather.

1. Cause and effect (السبب والنتيجة)

The cause is why something happens; the effect is what happens. A thunderstorm (cause) leads to a power cut (effect).

WordPatternExample
because / since / as+ clause (cause)She stayed inside because it was raining.
because of / due to+ noun (cause)She stayed inside due to the bad weather.
socause + , so + effectThe thunderstorm struck, so there was no power.
therefore / as a result / consequentlycause. Therefore, + effectMary heard a hurricane warning. Therefore, she looked for a safe place.

Think of a chain: cause -> effect, and each effect can become the next cause: heavy rain -> flooded roads -> traffic problems -> late to school.

2. Comparing (المقارنة)

A comparison paragraph shows similarities (what is the same) and differences (what is not). Choose two places or seasons and compare the same points: temperature, humidity, rain.

  • Similarities: both, similarly, likewise, also, as ... as.
  • Differences: however, whereas, while, but, on the other hand, unlike, comparatives (hotter, drier).

Organisation: point by point (temperature of A and B, then humidity of A and B) is the clearest. Block method: all about A, then all about B.

Example 1 (cause and effect). "Heavy rain fell on the city. As a result, many roads were flooded. Because of the flooding, schools closed, so students stayed home." Causes and effects: rain -> floods -> school closure.
Example 2 (comparison paragraph). "Dubai and Al Ain both have a hot desert climate, and both have very hot summers. However, Dubai is on the coast, so it is more humid, whereas Al Ain is inland and drier. Similarly, both cities have mild winters, but the nights in Al Ain are cooler. Overall, the two cities are similar in temperature but different in humidity."
Point-by-point: temperature (similar), humidity (different), winter (similar), nights (different).
⚠️ (1) because + clause but because of + noun: "because of the rain", not "because of it rained". (2) so shows the result; because shows the reason: do not swap them ("It rained, because we stayed in" is wrong). (3) However and therefore need a full stop or semicolon before them and a comma after. (4) Whereas / while join two contrasting clauses in one sentence. (5) Compare the same point in both places.
Exam corner. Writing 1.2 (25 marks) often asks you to compare seasons or explain why something is good or bad. Linkers of cause and result earn marks for "cohesion". Adverbial linkers also appear in Part 2 grammar. Use at least two different cause/result words and one contrast word in your answer.

Quick summary

  • because/since + clause; because of/due to + noun; so/therefore/as a result = effect.
  • Compare: both, similarly (same); however, whereas, unlike (different).
  • Point-by-point organisation is clearer.
  • Punctuation: linker after a full stop, then a comma.
