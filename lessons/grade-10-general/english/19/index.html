Vocabulary: Come Rain or Shine

The idea in detail

These words belong to the theme "Come Rain or Shine" (in any weather). They are common in Part 1 and in reading texts about weather, safety and clothing.

Understanding the science

Dark colours absorb more of the sun's light and turn it into heat, so a black shirt feels hotter. Light colours reflect more sunlight, so a white shirt keeps you cooler. That is why many people in the UAE wear white or light-coloured clothes in the summer, and why walls of many houses are painted white. Light, loose cotton fabric also allows air to move and sweat to dry.

Temperature is a number for one moment; climate is the pattern over many years. We say "The temperature today is 41" but "the UAE has a hot desert climate".

Ability and skill: ability is the natural power (the ability to swim); skill is learned through practice (first-aid skills).

Example 1. "A white car stays cooler in the sun because its paint ____ sunlight." Answer: reflects. "A black car gets very hot because it ____ the heat." Answer: absorbs.
Example 2. "Driving in a sandstorm is ____ because you cannot see the road." Options: hazardous / humid / textile. Answer: hazardous (dangerous). Another: "The rescue team used special ____ to find people after the flood": the noun skill/skills fits better than "ability" when it is learned training.
⚠️ (1) absorb is the opposite of reflect (take in versus send back). (2) severe is an adjective (severe weather); safety is a noun. (3) climate is not the same as weather. (4) fabric is a type of cloth; textile is cloth or the industry. (5) Use "the ability to" + verb: "the ability to swim", not "ability for".
Exam corner. In Part 1, read the sentence for the logic: heat, light and colour clues show absorb or reflect; danger clues show hazardous or severe. For word-form gaps decide noun (safety), adjective (safe) or adverb. Use these words in your Writing 1.2 about seasons: "The climate of the UAE is hot, so the temperature in summer is very high."

Quick summary

  • temperature (now) versus climate (long term); humidity = water vapour.
  • Dark absorbs heat; light reflects it.
  • hazardous = dangerous; severe = extreme; safety = protection.
  • ability (power) versus skill (learned); rescue = save; textile/fabric = cloth.
