Listening: Weather Talk, Camping & History

Listening 1 – talking about the weather. Expressions: How come…? = why? · pouring = raining very heavily · tan = brown skin from the sun · chance of rain = possibility · degrees · sick of = tired of, bored with · weather forecast = prediction of the weather · freezing = very cold · warm, sunny, cloudy.

Reduced vs unreduced speech
Function words (to, and, you, can) are usually unstressed and reduced: wanna, gonna, gotta, "bread 'n' butter".
can (positive) is unstressed, short /kən/: I can SKI.
can't is stressed and not reduced: I CAN'T ski. Listen for the stress to know which one you heard.

The idea in detail

This lesson has three listening parts: a conversation about the weather, a camping story and a lecture about history and the environment.

Listening 1: talking about the weather

Useful expressions: How come...? (why?), pouring (raining very heavily), chance of rain (possibility), sick of (tired of), weather forecast (prediction of weather), freezing (very cold), tan (brown skin from the sun).

Reduced and unreduced speech. Function words (to, and, can, you) are usually unstressed and weak: "wanna", "gonna", "bread 'n' butter". Content words (nouns, verbs, adjectives) are stressed. The positive can is weak /kən/: "I can SKI" (the stress is on ski). The negative can't is stressed and clear: "I CAN'T ski". So you hear the difference through stress, not only the sound.

Listening 2: the camping story

Friends went hiking in summer clothes when the weather was sunny and clear. Then it started pouring. They went into the tent to change into dry clothes and left their wet clothes outside. Later, two bears came and took the clothes. They were scared and had no dry clothes. Skill: put events in order and retell the story.

Listening 3: lecture "Learning from History"

When humans started farming, they began to control the planet: farmers cut down trees and cleared land. Results: food was easier to get and people had time for other activities, but land was cleared for towns and people hunted animals until they became extinct (no longer exist). Lesson: we can work together to protect each other and the environment. Words: ancestors (family members who lived long ago), polluted, destroyed, protect.

Listening skills

(1) Predict from the title. (2) Note signal words such as first, then, later, but, finally (for order). (3) In a lecture, listen to the introduction and conclusion for the main idea; examples are details. (4) Use notes: short words, numbers, arrows.

Example 1 (order of events). Sunny hike -> sudden heavy rain -> change into dry clothes in the tent -> wet clothes left outside -> bears take the clothes -> friends scared with nothing dry. A retelling: "First, the friends hiked in the sun. Then it started to pour, so they changed in the tent. Later, two bears took their clothes."
Example 2 (lecture notes). Farming -> cut trees, clear land -> + more food, more free time; - towns built, animals hunted, extinct. Main idea: humans changed the planet, so we should cooperate to protect it.
⚠️ (1) Do not confuse "can" and "can't": listen for stress. (2) "Tan" here means brown skin, not a colour only. (3) "How come" means "why", not "how did you come". (4) Extinct is not the same as endangered (in danger). (5) Do not mix the main idea with an example (extinct animals is an example).
Exam corner. The vocabulary (pouring, freezing, forecast, extinct, protect) appears in Part 1 and Part 3 texts. The sequencing skill is the same as for "overall meaning" and "connection" questions. In writing, use the past tenses of lesson 14 to retell a story.

Quick summary

  • Function words are weak; can is weak, can't is stressed.
  • Camping story: sun -> rain -> change clothes -> bears -> scared.
  • Lecture: farming changed land and animals; extinct; protect the environment.
  • Main idea in introduction and conclusion; examples are details.
