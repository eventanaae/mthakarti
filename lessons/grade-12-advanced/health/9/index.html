2.1 What are diseases and disorders?

🩺 العلوم الصحية🎓 الصف الثاني عشر متقدم📌 هيكل الامتحان: Unit 2 · Lesson 2.1 · p34 · HSC.5.3.01.001

🎯 ذاكر الدرس وحل الكويز مجانًا في مذاكرتي
Disease (مرض): a medical condition that stops the body from working normally.
Disorder (اضطراب): the disruption of the normal functions of the body because of the presence of a disease.

Diseases can cause pain, can stop parts of the body working properly and, in some cases, death. They can affect any part or system of the body.

Communicable (معدٍ)Non-communicable (غير معدٍ)
Can be spread or passed from person to personCannot be passed from person to person
influenza, COVID-19, gastroenteritis, malaria, pneumoniaepilepsy, hypertension, diabetes, multiple sclerosis, heart disease
Acute (حاد)Chronic (مزمن)
Temporary / short-term, usually less than three monthsLong-term, three months or more
common cold, pneumonia, influenza, feverarthritis, diabetes, asthma, allergies
  • sign – can be observed by other people (skin rash, cough).
  • symptom – a feeling or change experienced by the patient (pain).
⚠️ Malaria is communicable even though it is spread by mosquitoes, not directly by touch. A disease can be both chronic and non-communicable (diabetes) or acute and communicable (flu).
