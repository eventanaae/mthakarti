2.3 Alzheimer's, Parkinson's & sciatica

🩺 العلوم الصحية🎓 الصف الثاني عشر متقدم📌 هيكل الامتحان: Unit 2 · Lesson 2.3

🎯 ذاكر الدرس وحل الكويز مجانًا في مذاكرتي

Alzheimer's disease

  • Wasting away and death of brain cells → memory loss; symptoms get worse over time (degenerative).
  • Leads to confusion, behaviour changes, difficulty speaking, walking and eating.
  • The most common cause of dementia; affects people aged 65 and above; women more than men; risk increases with family history.
  • Signs/symptoms: difficulty remembering newly-learned information, forgetting names and everyday things, getting lost in familiar places, difficulty following conversations, difficulty concentrating, depression or mood swings.
  • Treatment: no cure; medication can slow the destruction of brain cells; group therapy to improve memory; story boards of past events using photos or music (e.g. a wedding day).
Dementia is a condition (a group of symptoms); Alzheimer's is a disease that causes it.

Parkinson's disease

  • Degenerative disease: nerve cells in the brain that make dopamine are lost.
  • Signs: tremor (shaking), slow movement, stiff muscles, poor balance.
  • Usually affects people over 60; no cure – medication and physiotherapy help.

Sciatica

  • Pain caused by pressure on the sciatic nerve, felt from the lower back down the buttock and leg, often with numbness or tingling.
  • Often caused by a slipped (herniated) disc; usually improves within weeks with painkillers, exercise and physiotherapy.
⚠️ Tremor → Parkinson's; memory loss → Alzheimer's; seizures → epilepsy; leg pain from the back → sciatica.
