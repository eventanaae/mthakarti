1.2 Suffixes

🩺 العلوم الصحية🎓 الصف الثاني عشر متقدم📌 هيكل الامتحان: Unit 1 · Lesson 1.2 · p18

🎯 ذاكر الدرس وحل الكويز مجانًا في مذاكرتي
A suffix (لاحقة) is added to the end of a word. Suffixes describe a condition, procedure or disease.
SuffixMeaningExample
-itisinflammation (التهاب)arthritis, tonsillitis
-ologystudy ofcardiology
-ologistspecialist inneurologist
-algiapainmyalgia (muscle pain)
-aemia (-emia)blood conditionanaemia
-ectomysurgical removalappendectomy
-otomycutting into (incision)tracheotomy
-ostomymaking an openingcolostomy
-scopyvisual examinationendoscopy
-grama record / pictureelectrocardiogram (ECG)
-megalyenlargementcardiomegaly
-omatumour, swellingcarcinoma
-pathydiseaseneuropathy
-osisabnormal conditionosteoporosis
-plastysurgical repairrhinoplasty
tonsill + ectomy = tonsillectomy = surgical removal of the tonsils.
hepat + itis = hepatitis = inflammation of the liver.
⚠️ -ectomy (remove) ≠ -otomy (cut into) ≠ -ostomy (make an opening).

اختبر نفسك: أسئلة على 1.2 Suffixes

Which suffix means 'inflammation'?

✅ الجواب: -itis

-itis = inflammation.

'Appendectomy' means:

✅ الجواب: Surgical removal of the appendix

-ectomy = surgical removal.

A specialist in the nervous system is called a:

✅ الجواب: neurologist

-ologist = specialist; -ology = study of.
