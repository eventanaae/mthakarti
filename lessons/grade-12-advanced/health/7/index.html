1.4 Responding to medical instructions

🩺 العلوم الصحية🎓 الصف الثاني عشر متقدم📌 هيكل الامتحان: Unit 1 · Lesson 1.4

🎯 ذاكر الدرس وحل الكويز مجانًا في مذاكرتي
Knowing common medical terms helps you respond correctly to instructions: when speaking to a doctor, in a first aid situation, on the phone with a healthcare professional, or if you become a healthcare professional.
TermMeaning
acuteshort-term, usually less than three months
chroniclong-term, usually lasts three months or longer
signsomething others can observe or measure (rash, cough)
symptomsomething only the patient feels (pain, nausea)
diagnosisidentifying the disease (تشخيص)
prognosisthe likely outcome of the disease (توقع سير المرض)
treatment / curecare given / complete recovery
remissioncondition is better, but the patient is not cured
terminalan illness that cannot be cured
infectiouscan be passed from person to person
biopsyremoving a small tissue sample for testing

Visiting the doctor

  • Before: think about the reason for your visit; list what to talk about.
  • During: describe all signs and symptoms, explain your medical history, say which body parts are affected, ask about anything unclear, note the recommendations.
  • After: check your questions were answered and that you understand the advice; if not, ask again.

In an emergency: stay calm, explain clearly, listen carefully to instructions, ask if you do not understand.

⚠️ Diagnosis = what the disease is; prognosis = what will happen. Remission ≠ cure.

اختبر نفسك: أسئلة على 1.4 Responding to medical instructions

A disease that lasts three months or longer is described as:

✅ الجواب: chronic

Chronic = long-term (three months or more).

A patient's condition is better, but they are not cured. This is called:

✅ الجواب: remission

Remission = improvement without cure.

'Prognosis' means:

✅ الجواب: The likely outcome of a disease

Prognosis predicts the outcome; diagnosis identifies the disease.
