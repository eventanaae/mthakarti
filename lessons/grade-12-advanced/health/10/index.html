2.2 Diseases of the musculoskeletal system

🩺 العلوم الصحية🎓 الصف الثاني عشر متقدم📌 هيكل الامتحان: Unit 2 · Lesson 2.2

🎯 ذاكر الدرس وحل الكويز مجانًا في مذاكرتي
The musculoskeletal system (الجهاز العضلي الهيكلي) = bones, joints, muscles, tendons and ligaments. It supports the body, protects organs and allows movement.
ConditionWhat is it?Key facts
ArthritisInflammation of the joints (arthr = joint, -itis = inflammation)Chronic; pain, stiffness and swelling in joints
OsteoarthritisWear and tear of the cartilage in jointsMore common with age; knees, hips, hands
Rheumatoid arthritisAn autoimmune disease: the immune system attacks the jointsAlso listed under immune system diseases
OsteoporosisBones lose density and become weak and brittle (oste = bone, -osis = condition)Higher risk in older people, especially women after menopause; bones break (fracture) easily

Treatment and prevention

  • Arthritis: no cure; pain-relief and anti-inflammatory medication, physiotherapy, staying active, healthy weight; joint replacement in severe cases.
  • Osteoporosis: calcium and vitamin D, weight-bearing exercise, not smoking, medication to strengthen bones.
⚠️ Osteoarthritis is a joint problem; osteoporosis is a bone-density problem.
