1.1 Introduction to medical terminology

🩺 العلوم الصحية🎓 الصف الثاني عشر متقدم📌 هيكل الامتحان: Unit 1 · Lesson 1.1 · p6

🎯 ذاكر الدرس وحل الكويز مجانًا في مذاكرتي
Medical terminology (المصطلحات الطبية) is the special language used by medical professionals. Its words are widely understood by any healthcare professional who speaks English.

Keywords

  • medical complaint – a pain or sickness in the body (شكوى مرضية).
  • terminology – the special words or phrases used in a particular field.
  • treatment – medical care given to a patient for an illness or injury (علاج).
  • emergency – a serious, unexpected, often dangerous situation requiring immediate action (طوارئ).
Why it matters for healthcare professionalsWhy it matters for you
Allows them to communicate clearly with each otherYou understand your doctor when they use medical terms
Understood by all healthcare professionalsYou understand your own illness
Describes the condition of a patientYou can explain an emergency to the emergency services
Identifies what treatment is neededUseful if you study a healthcare course at university
Saves time in an emergency
⚠️ The rationale question: medical terminology exists so that professionals communicate clearly, quickly and without misunderstanding – not to make medicine sound difficult.
