1.2 Prefixes

🩺 العلوم الصحية🎓 الصف الثاني عشر متقدم📌 هيكل الامتحان: Unit 1 · Lesson 1.2 · p16

🎯 ذاكر الدرس وحل الكويز مجانًا في مذاكرتي
A prefix (بادئة) is a letter or group of letters added to the beginning of a word to change its meaning. It often tells us number, position, size, speed or amount.
PrefixMeaningExample
hyper-too much, above normalhypertension
hypo-too little, below normalhypoglycaemia
tachy-fasttachycardia (fast heartbeat)
brady-slowbradycardia (slow heartbeat)
a-, an-without, absence ofanaemia
dys-difficult, painful, abnormaldyspnoea (difficult breathing)
peri-aroundpericarditis
endo-, intra-within, insideendoscopy, intravenous
inter-betweenintercostal (between ribs)
sub-under, belowsubcutaneous
poly-many, muchpolyuria
anti-againstantibiotic
pre- / post-before / afterpost-operative
bi- / hemi-two / halfbilateral, hemiplegia
⚠️ hyper- vs hypo- and inter- vs intra- are the classic traps. hyper = above, hypo = below; inter = between, intra = within.

اختبر نفسك: أسئلة على 1.2 Prefixes

What is a prefix?

✅ الجواب: Letters added to the beginning of a word to change its meaning

A prefix comes at the beginning of a word.

A heart rate that is too FAST is called:

✅ الجواب: tachycardia

tachy- = fast; brady- = slow.

'Hypoglycaemia' means:

✅ الجواب: Too little sugar in the blood

hypo- = below normal / too little.
