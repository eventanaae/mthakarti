Grammar 4: Present Simple Passive

📘 اللغة الإنجليزية🎓 الصف الثاني عشر متقدم📌 هيكل الامتحان: Part 2 · Present simple passive

🎯 ذاكر الدرس وحل الكويز مجانًا في مذاكرتي
Present simple passive = am / is / are + past participle (+ by + agent).
Use it for processes, facts and instructions when the action is more important than who does it.
ActivePassive
Workers cover the roof with a waterproof layer.The roof is covered with a waterproof layer.
The plants absorb rainwater.Rainwater is absorbed by the plants.
Builders plant low vegetation.Low vegetation is planted on top.
People install living roofs in many cities.Living roofs are installed in many cities.

Describing a process: First, the roof is checked for strength. Then a waterproof layer is added. Next, a drainage layer is laid, and soil is spread over it. Finally, small plants are planted.

Negative and question: Chemicals are not used. Is the roof watered every day?

⚠️ Match is/are to the subject: 'Plants are planted', 'Soil is spread'. Use the past participle, not the base verb: 'is absorb' ✗ → 'is absorbed' ✓. Irregular participles: lay → laid, grow → grown, build → built, spread → spread.

The idea in detail

In an active sentence the subject does the action: "Workers cover the roof." In a passive sentence the subject receives the action: "The roof is covered." We use the passive when the action or result is more important than who does it, or when the doer is unknown or obvious. It is very common in descriptions of processes and in scientific or formal writing.

Present simple passive: subject + am / is / are + past participle (+ by + agent).
Negative: is / are + not + past participle. Question: Is / Are + subject + past participle?

How to change active to passive (3 steps)

  1. Take the object of the active sentence and make it the new subject.
  2. Use is / are (matching the new subject) + the past participle of the main verb.
  3. Add by + doer only if it is important.

Example: People install living roofs. → (1) Living roofs ... (2) Living roofs are installed ... → Living roofs are installed. The agent "by people" is unnecessary.

Why / how it works

Think about a recipe or a factory: "First the eggs are mixed, then the flour is added". You do not care who mixes; you care about the steps. Describing how a living roof is built is the same. The main verb moves to the past participle (the "third form": cover - covered, lay - laid, build - built, grow - grown, spread - spread, plant - planted, water - watered) and "be" takes the tense (present simple: am, is, are).

Process text (sequence). First, the roof is checked for strength. Then a waterproof layer is added. Next, a drainage layer is laid, and soil is spread over it. Finally, small plants are planted. Use sequencing adverbs (Lesson 12) to organise the steps.

Worked example 1

Change to the passive: "Builders plant low vegetation on the roof."
Step 1: object = low vegetation → new subject.
Step 2: "vegetation" is uncountable, so singular → is; participle of plant = planted.
Step 3: agent (builders) not needed.
Low vegetation is planted on the roof.

Worked example 2

Complete the process with the verbs in brackets in the present simple passive:
"First, the roof ____ (check). Then a waterproof layer ____ (add). Next, soil ____ (spread). Finally, small plants ____ (plant)."
Subjects: roof (singular → is), layer (is), soil (is), plants (plural → are).
is checked / is added / is spread / are planted.
Negative: "Chemicals ____ (not use)" → are not used. Question: "____ the roof ____ (water) every day?" → Is the roof watered every day?

Common mistakes

⚠️ 1) Using the base verb: "is absorb" ✗ → "is absorbed" ✓. 2) Wrong "be": "Plants is planted" ✗ → "Plants are planted" ✓. 3) Wrong participle of irregular verbs: "is layed" ✗ → "is laid"; "is growed" ✗ → "is grown". 4) Passive question order: "Does the roof watered?" ✗ → "Is the roof watered?" ✓. 5) Using the passive when it is not needed, e.g. with intransitive verbs ("Rain is falling" cannot be passive).

Exam corner

How it is asked: "Part 2 - Present simple passive." Gap-fill: "Small plants ____ in the top layer of soil" - options are planted / is planted / plants. Full marks: (1) Is the subject receiving the action? (2) Check singular/plural for is/are. (3) Choose the past participle (watch irregular ones). (4) Negative: are not + participle.

Quick summary

  • Passive = am/is/are + past participle.
  • Used for processes, facts and when the doer is unimportant.
  • Agent with "by" only if needed.
  • Irregular participles: laid, grown, built, spread, made.
  • Question: Is/Are + subject + participle?
