Part 4B: The Comparative Essay (220+ words)

📘 اللغة الإنجليزية🎓 الصف الثاني عشر متقدم📌 هيكل الامتحان: Part 4 · FL.26 Comparing & contrasting

🎯 ذاكر الدرس وحل الكويز مجانًا في مذاكرتي

The essay compares and contrasts two things, e.g. a traditional home vs a smart home, or living in a megacity vs a small town. Write at least 220 words.

OrganisationHow it works
Point-by-pointEach body paragraph compares ONE feature for both topics (cost: A vs B; lifestyle: A vs B; environment: A vs B).
BlockOne paragraph about topic A, then one paragraph about topic B, ending with a comparison.

Structure: Introduction (hook + briefly describe both topics + thesis) → 2–3 body paragraphs (topic sentence, comparison, examples) → Conclusion (summarise similarities and differences + final judgment).

SimilarityDifference
both, similarly, likewise, in the same way, also, as ... as, the same aswhereas, while, unlike, in contrast, on the other hand, however, differ from, comparatives (cheaper, more crowded)
'Unlike small towns, megacities offer a wide range of jobs. However, they suffer from more congestion and pollution. Both places, though, can offer a good quality of life.'
'A smart home is more cost-effective to run than a traditional home, whereas a traditional home offers more privacy.'
Checklist: transitions added; grammar, spelling and punctuation checked; specific details included; sentence variety; formal style (no contractions).
⚠️ 'Unlike' is followed by a noun ('Unlike small towns, ...'), not a clause. Make sure you write about BOTH topics in every comparison.

The idea in detail

The second writing task is a comparative essay of at least 220 words. In a comparative essay you show how two things are similar (compare) and how they are different (contrast). The topics in the specifications are Smart Homes / Comparing Places, for example a traditional home vs a smart home or living in a megacity vs a small town.

Plan before you write (5 minutes)

  1. Choose 2-3 points of comparison (cost, lifestyle, environment, privacy, convenience).
  2. For each point, write one idea for topic A and one for topic B.
  3. Decide the structure: point-by-point or block.
StructureParagraphsBest for
Point-by-pointIntroduction; Body 1: cost (A vs B); Body 2: lifestyle (A vs B); Body 3: environment (A vs B); Conclusionthe clearest way to compare directly
BlockIntroduction; Body 1: all about A; Body 2: all about B (comparing back to A); Conclusionshort essays with few points

Why / how it works

Why 220 words? A short essay can have: Introduction (35-45 words), 2-3 body paragraphs (50-60 words each) and a conclusion (30-40 words). That makes 220-250 words, which is the length you need.

Language of comparison. Similarity: both, similarly, likewise, in the same way, also, as ... as, the same as. Difference: whereas, while, unlike, in contrast, on the other hand, however, differ from, comparatives (cheaper, more crowded). Add linkers and sequencing words from Lesson 12 as well.

Formal style. No contractions (do not = don't), no slang, no "I think" more than once. Use specific details: not "it is good" but "it saves about 20% of electricity".

Worked example 1 (model essay, point-by-point, about 230 words)

Traditional Homes and Smart Homes
Technology has changed the way we live. A traditional home depends on manual control, whereas a smart home uses automation to control lights, locks and air conditioning. This essay compares the two in terms of cost, comfort and privacy.
Firstly, the two types of home differ in cost. A smart home is more expensive to build because sensors and devices must be installed. However, it is more cost-effective in the long term, since it switches off appliances automatically and reduces utility bills. A traditional home is cheaper at the start, but the owner may pay more for energy each month.
Secondly, they differ in comfort. In a smart home, the owner can control the house remotely from a phone, which is very convenient. Unlike a smart home, a traditional home needs the owner to be present to switch things on and off. Both types of home, however, can be comfortable if they are well designed.
Finally, privacy is an important difference. A traditional home offers more privacy because there are no cameras or microphones connected to databases. In contrast, a smart home collects information about daily routines, which may be a risk.
In conclusion, both homes have advantages. A smart home is more convenient and cost-effective, whereas a traditional home gives more privacy. The best choice therefore depends on what each family values most.

Worked example 2 (useful sentences, megacity vs small town)

Similarity: "Both megacities and small towns can offer a good quality of life."
Difference: "Unlike small towns, megacities offer a wide range of jobs; however, they suffer from more congestion and pollution."
Conclusion: "To sum up, a megacity is more exciting and full of opportunities, while a small town is calmer and cheaper."

Common mistakes

⚠️ 1) Writing about only one topic in a paragraph and not comparing. 2) Fewer than 220 words. 3) No paragraphs or no linkers. 4) "Unlike" + clause ✗ → "Unlike" + noun ("Unlike small towns, ..."). 5) Contractions and informal language. 6) "more cheaper" - double comparative. 7) No conclusion, or a conclusion that adds new ideas.

Exam corner

How it is asked: "Part 4 - FL.26 Comparing and contrasting." Marks are given for: content (both topics, clear points), organisation (introduction, body, conclusion, linkers), language (accuracy and variety: comparatives, passive, conditionals), and length. Checklist at the end: transitions added; grammar, spelling and punctuation checked; specific details; variety of sentences; formal style; at least 220 words.

Quick summary

  • At least 220 words; compare AND contrast.
  • Structure: introduction (hook + both topics + thesis), 2-3 body paragraphs, conclusion.
  • Point-by-point or block organisation.
  • Similarity: both, similarly, likewise; difference: whereas, unlike, in contrast, however.
  • Formal style and a final check.

Part 4.2: 'Write an essay comparing two places.' How many words should you write at least?

✅ الجواب: 220

The task requires at least 220 words in paragraphs.

Which sentence is the best thesis for a comparative essay on smart homes and traditional homes?

✅ الجواب: Although smart homes are more convenient, traditional homes offer greater privacy.

A thesis states the main comparison clearly: here, a contrast between convenience and privacy.

Which linker is best for comparing two things that are SIMILAR?

✅ الجواب: Similarly

Similarly (like both, likewise) introduces similarities.
