SpacePlanetary rovers, robotic arms on space stations, landers
Why robots in space? Space is dangerous for humans: extreme temperatures, radiation, no breathable air, dust and rough terrain. Robots can work there for years, cost less than human missions and take no risk with human life.
  • Communication delay: radio signals between Earth and Mars take several minutes each way, so rovers cannot be driven in real time like a toy car – they need autonomy (e.g. autonomous navigation and hazard avoidance).
  • Mars rovers (NASA): Sojourner, Spirit, Opportunity, Curiosity, Perseverance.
  • UAE: the Rashid rover (Emirates Lunar Mission, Mohammed Bin Rashid Space Centre) was designed to explore the Moon; the Hope Probe orbits Mars to study its atmosphere.
⚠️ The Hope Probe is an orbiter, not a rover. Rashid is a lunar (Moon) rover.
