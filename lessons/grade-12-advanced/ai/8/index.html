Chapter 2 – Modelling a chassis and suspension system

  • Use CAD software to draw the chassis and suspension in 3D, test fit and movement before building.
  • Build a prototype (3D printing, laser cutting, kits) and test it on rough ground and slopes.
  • Choose materials that are strong and light; check stability (centre of gravity) and that all wheels touch the ground.
STREAM project: Design and manufacture of a smart controlled Space Rover – seven stages:
  1. Design brief – a short statement of the problem and what must be made.
  2. Analysis – break the brief into questions and requirements (specification).
  3. Research – existing rovers, materials, components, users and conditions.
  4. Possible solutions – sketch several different ideas.
  5. Final design – choose and develop the best idea with dimensions and parts.
  6. Design realisation – manufacture, assemble, wire and program the rover.
  7. Evaluation – test against the specification and suggest improvements.

"Smart" control – a microcontroller reads sensors (e.g. an ultrasonic distance sensor to avoid obstacles) and controls the motors; it may also be remote controlled.

Power check: motors drawing 2 A from a 12 V battery use P = V × I = 12 × 2 = 24 W.
⚠️ Evaluation compares the finished product with the specification – not just "does it look good".
