Suspension, rocker-bogie & chassis stabilisation

🤖 الذكاء الاصطناعي والتكنولوجيا🎓 الصف الثاني عشر متقدم📌 هيكل الامتحان: Ch1 · S2 · Suspension, rocker-bogie, stabilisation

🎯 ذاكر الدرس وحل الكويز مجانًا في مذاكرتي
Suspension (نظام التعليق) connects the wheels to the body. It keeps the wheels in contact with the ground, absorbs bumps and keeps the body stable.

Rocker-bogie suspension – used on NASA Mars rovers (e.g. Sojourner, Spirit, Opportunity, Curiosity, Perseverance):

  • Six wheels; on each side a large link called the rocker carries one wheel and a smaller link called the bogie, which carries two wheels.
  • Links pivot freely – there are no springs, so the rover does not bounce.
  • All six wheels stay in contact with uneven ground, sharing the load and giving good traction when climbing over rocks.
  • Usually designed for slow driving speeds.

Chassis stabilisation

  • The left and right rockers are joined through a differential (a bar or gears). When one side goes up, the other goes down, and the body tilts by only the average of the two – keeping the body and its instruments more level.
  • A low centre of gravity and a wide wheel base reduce the risk of tipping over on slopes.
Spring suspension (cars)Rocker-bogie (rovers)
Absorbs bumps at high speed; uses springs/dampersNo springs; passive links; slow, very stable on rocky ground
⚠️ The bogie has two wheels; the rocker has one wheel plus the bogie. The differential keeps the body level.
