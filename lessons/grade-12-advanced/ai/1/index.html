Introduction to robotics (recap)

🎯 ذاكر الدرس وحل الكويز مجانًا في مذاكرتي
A robot (روبوت) is a programmable machine that can sense its environment, process information (think/decide) and act on the world, often with some autonomy.

Sense → Think → Act cycle:

  • Sense – sensors collect data (distance, light, temperature, cameras, touch).
  • Think – the controller (microcontroller/computer) runs the program and decides.
  • Act – actuators (motors, servos) create movement or other outputs.
Main partRole
Structure / chassis (الهيكل)Holds and protects all parts
Sensors (الحساسات)Inputs: measure the environment
Controller (وحدة التحكم)Processes inputs and sends commands
Actuators (المشغلات)Outputs: produce motion
Power supplyBatteries, solar panels, etc.

Levels of control: teleoperated (a human controls it remotely), semi-autonomous and autonomous (it makes its own decisions using sensors and AI).

⚠️ A sensor is an input; a motor is an output (actuator). Do not mix them up.

What is the role of the controller in a robot?

✅ الجواب: It processes sensor data and sends commands to the actuators

The controller is the 'brain' that runs the program.
