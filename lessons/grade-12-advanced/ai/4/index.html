Vehicle design specifications: the space rover body

🤖 الذكاء الاصطناعي والتكنولوجيا🎓 الصف الثاني عشر متقدم📌 هيكل الامتحان: Ch1 · S2 · Space rover body

🎯 ذاكر الدرس وحل الكويز مجانًا في مذاكرتي
A design specification (مواصفات التصميم) is a list of measurable requirements the rover must meet. Engineers write it before designing.
RequirementWhy it matters on another planet
Mass & sizeLaunch cost and the space inside the rocket/lander are limited
TerrainRocks, sand, slopes and craters need good traction and suspension
TemperatureElectronics must be kept warm in a protected, insulated body
PowerSolar panels (need sunlight, affected by dust) or a radioisotope generator
CommunicationAntennas to Earth or via orbiters; signal delay
PayloadCameras, scientific instruments, robotic arm
ReliabilityNo one can repair it – parts must last

The body (chassis) carries the computer, batteries and instruments, protects electronics from dust and temperature, and provides mounting points for the suspension, wheels, mast and arm.

Gravity check: on Mars, g ≈ 3.7 m/s². A 100 kg rover weighs W = m × g = 100 × 3.7 = 370 N on Mars, but about 100 × 9.8 = 980 N on Earth. Its mass stays 100 kg.
⚠️ Mass does not change between planets; weight does.

What is a design specification?

✅ الجواب: A list of measurable requirements the product must meet

Specifications guide and test the design.

Why is mass a key specification for a space rover?

✅ الجواب: Launching mass is expensive and space in the lander is limited

Every kilogram increases launch cost.

A rover has a mass of 200 kg. Using g = 3.7 m/s² on Mars, what is its weight on Mars?

✅ الجواب: 740 N

W = m × g = 200 × 3.7 = 740 N.
