Drivetrains, steering and gears

🤖 الذكاء الاصطناعي والتكنولوجيا🎓 الصف الثاني عشر متقدم📌 هيكل الامتحان: Ch1 · S2 · Drivetrains & steering

🎯 ذاكر الدرس وحل الكويز مجانًا في مذاكرتي
The drivetrain (نظام نقل الحركة) is the set of parts that transfers power from the motors to the wheels: motors, gears/gearboxes, shafts and wheels.
  • Number of driven wheels: 2WD, 4WD or 6WD. More driven wheels → better traction on loose or rough ground. Mars rovers usually have six wheels, each with its own motor.
  • Gear reduction: a small driver gear turning a big driven gear gives more torque, less speed – ideal for climbing.
Gear ratio = teeth on driven gear ÷ teeth on driver gear
Driver 10 teeth, driven 40 teeth → ratio 4 : 1.
Motor 200 rpm → wheel 200 ÷ 4 = 50 rpm; torque is multiplied by about 4 (ignoring losses).
Distance: wheel diameter 0.2 m → circumference π × 0.2 ≈ 0.63 m; 10 turns ≈ 6.3 m.
Steering typeHow it works
Skid (differential) steeringWheels on the left and right turn at different speeds or directions; simple, but wheels skid
Ackermann steeringFront wheels turn at different angles (like a car); needs a turning radius
Independent (explicit) steeringCorner wheels have their own steering motors; the rover can turn on the spot (point turn)
⚠️ Bigger driven gear = slower but stronger. To turn on the spot, a rover needs independent steering or skid steering with sides turning in opposite directions.

What is a drivetrain?

✅ الجواب: The parts that transfer power from the motors to the wheels

Motors, gears, shafts and wheels.

A 10-tooth driver gear turns a 50-tooth driven gear. What is the gear ratio?

✅ الجواب: 5 : 1

Ratio = driven ÷ driver = 50 ÷ 10 = 5.

A motor spins at 300 rpm and drives the wheel through a 3 : 1 gear reduction. What is the wheel speed?

✅ الجواب: 100 rpm

300 ÷ 3 = 100 rpm.
