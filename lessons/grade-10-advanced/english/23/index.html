The idea in detail

The Task 1.2 essay is based on one of three themes: Health Clubs, Talking about Stress or Screens Everywhere! You do not know which one you will get, so prepare ideas for all three. For each theme you should have: 2 benefits, 2 problems, 1 solution and 1 personal example. With this bank of ideas you can write a good essay quickly.

How to turn ideas into an essay

Use the structure: introduction (opinion) → reason 1 + example → reason 2 + example → problem/other view + solution → conclusion. Link with however, for example, therefore, as a result. Connect your ideas to your own life (a personal example) because it makes your writing specific and convincing.

Model paragraph 1 (Screens). Firstly, screens improve productivity because students can research and share work quickly. However, using them for too long can harm health. For example, tired eyes and poor sleep are common among teenagers. Therefore, I believe young people should limit their screen time and take regular breaks.
Model paragraph 2 (Stress). In my opinion, exams are the main cause of stress for students. As a result, many feel nervous and cannot sleep well. One effective solution is to make a study plan and to talk to a friend or teacher. For example, when I split my revision into small parts, I felt calmer and more confident.
Model paragraph 3 (Health club). Joining a health club has clear benefits. It keeps students fit and improves their mood. On the other hand, membership can be expensive. Therefore, schools should offer cheaper programmes so that every student can take part.
Analysis: each paragraph has a reason, an explanation, an example or result and a linking word; the tone is formal; the content answers a prompt.
⚠️ Common mistakes: (1) Writing a long essay about a different point of the theme; stay on the three prompts. (2) Memorising a whole essay and using it for the wrong prompt. (3) Using slang (guys, stuff, kids). (4) Having ideas without examples. (5) Forgetting the solution if the prompt asks for one.
Exam corner: Part 4 writing themes: Health Clubs, Talking about Stress, Screens Everywhere! (25 marks for the essay). Read the three prompts carefully, tick them off as you write, and match each prompt with one paragraph. A flexible idea bank + the essay structure + correct language = full marks.
