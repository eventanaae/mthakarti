Functional Language: Comparing and Contrasting

🌦️ English🎓 الصف العاشر متقدم📌 هيكل الامتحان: Part 4 Writing - FL.26 Comparing and contrasting

FunctionWords and structuresExample
Similarityboth... and, like, similarly, in the same way, as well as, the same asBoth TV adverts and online adverts use slogans.
Differencehowever, but, whereas, while, on the other hand, in contrast, unlike, althoughTV ads are expensive, whereas social-media ads are cheap.
Comparatives-er than, more + adj + than, (not) as + adj + as, less... thanWalking is less stressful than driving.
Superlativesthe -est, the most + adjThis is the most informative programme.
Punctuation: "However," / "On the other hand," / "In contrast," start a sentence and are followed by a comma. Whereas / while / although join two clauses in one sentence.
"Unlike a health club, exercising at home is free. However, a club offers expert advice. Both options improve fitness."
⚠️ "more better" ✗ → "better". "different than" is informal; use "different from". "Unlike" is followed by a noun/pronoun, not a clause. Do not use but and although in the same clause.

The idea in detail

To compare means to show how things are similar; to contrast means to show how they are different. In writing, you need special words to link the two things clearly. This is useful for the essay (for example: a health club vs exercising at home; TV adverts vs social-media adverts) and for reading questions about two viewpoints.

How to use them

  • Short adjectives (one syllable): add -er / -est: fast → faster → the fastest. Double the final consonant if needed: big → bigger. Adjectives ending in -y: happy → happier.
  • Long adjectives (two or more syllables): use more / most: expensive → more expensive → the most expensive.
  • Irregular: good → better → the best; bad → worse → the worst.
  • Connectors at the start of a sentence (However, On the other hand, In contrast, Similarly) are followed by a comma.
  • Connectors inside a sentence (whereas, while, although) join two clauses: "A club is expensive, whereas exercising at home is free."
  • Unlike is followed by a noun or pronoun: "Unlike a health club, exercising at home is free."
Model paragraph (health club vs home exercise). Both a health club and exercising at home can improve fitness. However, they are different in several ways. A club has expert trainers and modern equipment, whereas at home you have only simple tools. On the other hand, home workouts are cheaper and more flexible. Unlike a club membership, which costs a monthly fee, exercising at home is free. Overall, a club is the better choice for beginners, but home exercise is the most convenient option for busy students.
Analysis: similarity (both... and), difference (However, whereas, On the other hand, Unlike), comparative (cheaper, more flexible, the better), superlative (the most convenient). Notice the commas after sentence-starting connectors.
⚠️ Common mistakes: (1) "more better" ✗ → "better" ✓ (never double comparatives). (2) "different than" is informal; write "different from". (3) Unlike + clause ✗ ("Unlike he is rich") ✓ "Unlike him,...". (4) but and although in the same clause. (5) Forgetting than in a comparison: "taller from" ✗. (6) Forgetting "the" before a superlative.
Exam corner: Part 4 Writing, FL.26 Comparing and contrasting. In the opinion essay, a prompt such as "compare two options" or "say which is better" needs both sides. Use at least two connectors (however, whereas) and one comparative; this raises your language range score. Give a clear final choice in the conclusion.

Quick summary

  • Similarity: both... and, similarly, like. Difference: however, whereas, unlike, on the other hand.
  • Short adjective + -er; long adjective + more; irregular: better, worse.
  • Commas after sentence-starting connectors.
  • Different from; no double comparatives.
