Grammar: Present Simple Passive

🌦️ English🎓 الصف العاشر متقدم📌 هيكل الامتحان: Part 2 Grammar - G.10.1 Passives (present simple passive)

🎯 ذاكر الدرس وحل الكويز مجانًا في مذاكرتي

Use the present simple passive when the action or the receiver is more important than who does it, or when the doer is unknown. News reports and processes use it a lot.

Form: subject + am / is / are + past participle (V3)
Negative: is/are + not + V3  |  Question: Is/Are + subject + V3?
ActivePassive
Police investigate the accident.The accident is investigated by police.
Doctors prescribe medicine.Medicine is prescribed by doctors.
They broadcast the news at 8 p.m.The news is broadcast at 8 p.m.
People watch commercials.Commercials are watched by millions.

Use by + agent only if the doer matters. Passive tip: the object of the active sentence becomes the subject.

"Passengers' injuries are treated at the hospital." (is/are agrees with the subject: injuries = are)
⚠️ 1) Agreement: The witness is asked / The witnesses are asked. 2) You need V3: "is invite" is wrong, "is invited" is right. 3) Do not use the passive with intransitive verbs (happen, arrive).

The idea in detail

English sentences are usually active: the doer (subject) does the action. "Police investigate the accident." In a passive sentence the receiver of the action becomes the subject: "The accident is investigated by police." We choose the passive when (a) the action or the receiver is more important than the doer, (b) we do not know who did it, or (c) the doer is obvious. News reports, science processes and rules use the passive a lot. (المبني للمجهول)

Form of the present simple passive

subject + am / is / are + past participle (V3)

  • Negative: subject + is/are not + V3: The report is not published.
  • Question: Is/Are + subject + V3? Are the passengers treated here?
  • Be agrees with the new subject: singular → is; plural → are; I → am.

How to change active to passive: 3 steps

  1. Take the object of the active sentence and make it the subject.
  2. Use is/are (matching the new subject) + V3 of the main verb.
  3. Put the old subject after by if it is important; otherwise leave it out.
ActivePassive
Nurses check the patients every hour.The patients are checked every hour.
A pilot flies the plane.The plane is flown by a pilot.
Doctors treat injuries.Injuries are treated by doctors.
Example 1. Change to the passive: "The police question the witnesses."
Step 1: object = the witnesses → new subject. Step 2: plural → are; V3 of question = questioned. Step 3: by the police (can be kept or dropped). Answer: The witnesses are questioned (by the police).
Example 2 (gap-fill). "In this hospital, all injuries ____ immediately." Options: A treat B are treated C is treated. Subject = injuries (plural, receives the action) → are treated. Answer: B.
⚠️ Common mistakes: (1) Wrong agreement: The witness is asked but The witnesses are asked. (2) Forgetting V3: "is invite" is wrong; "is invited" is right. (3) Using the passive with intransitive verbs (they have no object): never "is happened" or "is arrived". (4) Adding by + agent when it is unimportant or unknown. (5) Irregular V3: broadcast - broadcast, write - written, do - done.
Exam corner: Part 2 grammar, G.10.1 Passives (present simple passive), 2 marks per gap. Gap-fill often gives options like "treat / are treated / is treating". Ask: does the subject do the action or receive it? Receives → passive. Then check singular/plural. In the news-report text, a passive gap is often near words like "by", "injured", "taken", "reported".

Quick summary

  • Form: am/is/are + V3.
  • Use when the action or receiver matters more than the doer.
  • Object of the active sentence becomes the subject.
  • Check agreement (is/are) and use the correct V3.
  • Intransitive verbs (happen, arrive) have no passive.
