Writing 1.1: Four Main-Idea Sentences

🌦️ English🎓 الصف العاشر متقدم📌 هيكل الامتحان: Part 4 Writing 1.1 - ENG.07.WR.S.6.1 (15 marks, 170-word text, 3-4 sentences)

🎯 ذاكر الدرس وحل الكويز مجانًا في مذاكرتي

Task 1.1: Read a text of about 170 words and write its main ideas in your own words in 3-4 sentences (the student guide says four). 15 marks.

Four-sentence frame
1. Main idea: The text is mainly about... / The author explains that...
2. Important point: One important point is that... / The text also explains how...
3. Supporting idea: Another key idea is... / In addition, the author discusses...
4. Final idea: Finally, the text highlights... / Overall, the text shows that...
Text: screens affect sleep, eyes and mood; blue light delays sleep; experts suggest a screen-free hour before bed; limiting time improves focus.
Answer: The text is mainly about how too much screen time affects health. One important point is that the blue light from screens delays sleep. In addition, the author discusses how tired eyes and a poor mood can result. Finally, the text highlights that limiting screen time before bed improves focus.
  • Identify the most important ideas (usually one per paragraph).
  • Paraphrase: use synonyms and change the structure; do not copy full sentences.
  • No personal opinion, no small or unrelated details.
⚠️ Common mistakes: copying sentences; writing "I think..."; choosing a small example as a main idea; writing fewer than 3 or more than 4 sentences; mixing the order of ideas.

The idea in detail

Task 1.1 asks you to read a text of about 170 words and write its main ideas in your own words in 3-4 sentences (the student guide says four). It is worth 15 marks. This skill is called summarising or writing main ideas. You are not copying the text; you are showing that you understood it and can say it briefly.

Step by step

  1. Read the text once for general meaning, then again, underlining the most important idea in each paragraph.
  2. Choose four main ideas: the overall topic + three key points. Skip small examples and numbers.
  3. Paraphrase: change the words (synonyms) and the structure, not just a few words. Example: "Screens affect sleep" → "Using devices can disturb our rest".
  4. Write one idea per sentence in a logical order, using a frame.
  5. Check: 3-4 sentences, no opinions, no copied sentences, correct grammar.

The four-sentence frame

SentenceJobStarter
1Main ideaThe text is mainly about... / The author explains that...
2Important pointOne important point is that...
3Supporting ideaIn addition, the author discusses...
4Final ideaFinally, the text highlights that... / Overall, the text shows that...
Worked example. Source text (summary of ideas): Mobile phones are everywhere. Teenagers use them for hours. Studies show that late-night phone use makes it harder to fall asleep, and tired students concentrate less in class. Doctors suggest turning off phones an hour before bed and charging them outside the bedroom. Some schools also teach students how to manage their time online.
Step 1: main idea = phone use affects young people's sleep and learning. Step 2: key points = (a) late use disturbs sleep, (b) tiredness reduces concentration, (c) solutions: turn off phones before bed. Step 3: paraphrase and join.
Model answer: The text is mainly about how mobile phones affect teenagers' health and school life. One important point is that using a phone late at night makes it difficult to sleep. In addition, the author explains that tired students find it hard to concentrate in class. Finally, the text recommends switching off devices before bedtime to protect sleep.
Check: 4 sentences, no "I think", no copied sentences, all four ideas are in the order of the text.
⚠️ Common mistakes: (1) Copying full sentences from the text. (2) Giving your opinion ("I think phones are bad"). (3) Choosing a small example (the name of a school) as a main idea. (4) Writing only one or two sentences, or five or more. (5) Mixing the order of ideas or writing a very long sentence with many commas. (6) Spelling and tense mistakes.
Exam corner: Part 4, Task 1.1 (ENG.07.WR.S.6.1), 15 marks, 3-4 sentences. The marks reward: (a) correct main ideas, (b) your own words, (c) clear sentences and accurate grammar. To get full marks write exactly four clear sentences, each carrying a different main idea, and finish in about 8-10 minutes.

Quick summary

  • Write 3-4 sentences giving the main ideas in your own words.
  • Frame: main idea → important point → supporting idea → final idea.
  • Paraphrase; do not copy.
  • No opinions and no small details.
