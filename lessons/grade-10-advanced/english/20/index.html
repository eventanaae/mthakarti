Writing 1.2: The Opinion Essay (170+ words)

🌦️ English🎓 الصف العاشر متقدم📌 هيكل الامتحان: Part 4 Writing 1.2 - opinion essay, 3 prompts, at least 170 words (25 marks)

Task 1.2: "Write an opinion essay in which you ____: prompt 1, prompt 2, prompt 3. Write at least 170 words in paragraphs." 25 marks (rubric).

PartWhat to doUseful phrases
IntroductionIntroduce the topic and state your opinionIn my opinion, ... / I strongly believe that... / From my point of view...
Body paragraphsOne reason per paragraph + explanation + example; address all three promptsFirstly, ... / Another reason is... / For example, ... / Furthermore, ...
ConclusionRestate your opinion and finish clearlyIn conclusion, I believe that... / To sum up, ... / For these reasons, ...
Process: Plan → Write → Check. Plan 3 prompts + 1 reason each (2 min). Use linking words (firstly, because, however, finally). Count your words. Check spelling, tenses and punctuation.
Model (health club, short): In my opinion, joining a health club is a good idea for teenagers. Firstly, it keeps you fit. Regular exercise strengthens the body and improves sleep. For example, swimming three times a week raised my energy. Another reason is that it reduces stress; exercising helps you calm down after exams. Finally, clubs help you make friends who share the same goals. However, memberships can be expensive, so I believe schools should offer cheaper programmes. In conclusion, I strongly believe that health clubs improve teenagers' health and mood.
⚠️ Marks are lost for: missing a prompt, no paragraphs, fewer than 170 words, no opinion, no examples, many spelling/tense mistakes, and informal language (don't, gonna, lots of).

The idea in detail

Task 1.2 is an opinion essay: "Write an opinion essay in which you ... (three prompts). Write at least 170 words in paragraphs." It is worth 25 marks, the biggest single task in the exam. An opinion essay states what you think and supports it with reasons and examples. The three prompts tell you exactly what to cover, so each prompt must be answered.

Structure

PartJobPhrases
IntroductionIntroduce the topic and state your opinion (1-2 sentences)In my opinion, ... / I strongly believe that... / From my point of view...
Body (1-2 paragraphs)Reason + explanation + example for each promptFirstly, ... / Another reason is... / For example, ... / Furthermore, ...
ConclusionRestate your opinion in new wordsIn conclusion, ... / To sum up, ... / For these reasons, ...

The Plan - Write - Check process

  1. Plan (2-3 minutes): write the three prompts and one reason for each. Decide your opinion.
  2. Write (about 15 minutes): use linking words (firstly, because, however, finally, therefore). Use a P-E-E pattern in each paragraph: Point (reason), Explanation (why), Example.
  3. Check (3 minutes): count words (at least 170), check spelling, tenses and punctuation, and make sure all three prompts are covered.
Model essay (about 190 words). Prompts: give your opinion on how much time students spend on screens, give reasons, suggest a solution.
In my opinion, students should spend less time on screens in their free time. Firstly, too much screen time harms health. For example, many of my classmates have tired eyes and headaches because they play games until midnight. Sleep is important for the body, so late-night screens can reduce energy during the school day.
Another reason is that screens can reduce real communication. Although phones help us to chat with friends, face-to-face talking builds stronger friendships. Furthermore, when students check notifications all the time, their productivity falls and homework takes longer.
However, screens are not all bad. They allow us to research, learn languages and share work quickly. Therefore, I believe the best solution is balance: students should use screens for study, set a time limit, and switch off devices an hour before bed.
In conclusion, I strongly believe that young people can enjoy technology, but only if they control it. Balance protects both our health and our results.

Why this model scores well: it states an opinion, answers every prompt, has four clear paragraphs, uses linking words and examples, and keeps a formal tone.

⚠️ Common mistakes (marks lost): missing a prompt; one long block with no paragraphs; fewer than 170 words; no opinion; reasons with no examples; informal language (don't, gonna, lots of, kids); many spelling or tense errors; copying the prompts word for word; off-topic ideas.
Exam corner: Part 4 Task 1.2, 25 marks, rubric-marked (content, organisation, language accuracy and range). To reach the top band: cover all three prompts, organise in paragraphs, use varied vocabulary from the unit (stress, productivity, connectivity, fitness, remedy), and write at least 170 words. Aim for 180-200 so you stay safely above the minimum.

Quick summary

  • Introduction (opinion) - body (reason + example) - conclusion (restate).
  • Answer all three prompts, in paragraphs, 170+ words.
  • Plan, write, check; count words.
  • Formal tone; use linking words and examples.
