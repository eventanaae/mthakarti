Vocabulary: Infotainment (1)

🌦️ English🎓 الصف العاشر متقدم📌 هيكل الامتحان: Part 1 Vocabulary - connectivity, productivity, slogan, commercial, memorable

🎯 ذاكر الدرس وحل الكويز مجانًا في مذاكرتي

Infotainment = information + entertainment (news and media that inform and entertain).

WordMeaningExample
connectivity (n)the ability to connect to the internet or other devicesGood connectivity lets us stream videos anywhere.
productivity (n)how much work you do in a given timeTurning off notifications can raise your productivity.
slogan (n)a short, catchy phrase used by a company"Just do it" is a famous slogan.
commercial (n/adj)(n) a paid advertisement on TV or online; (adj) connected with businessThe commercial lasted thirty seconds.
memorable (adj)easy to remember; specialA memorable slogan stays in your mind.
Word parts: connect + -ivity (noun); product + -ive + -ity; memor(y) + -able = able to be remembered.
"The company's new ____ was short and ____, so everyone remembered it." = slogan, memorable.
⚠️ Commercial as a noun means an advert, not a shop. Connectivity is about connecting; do not confuse it with connection in a sentence needing the idea of ability or quality of connecting.

The idea in detail

Infotainment is a blend of two words: information + entertainment. It means media that both informs and entertains, such as news shows with fun graphics, or a documentary with music. Words in this theme are about media, advertising and technology.

  • connectivity (n): the ability of a device or a person to connect to the internet or to other devices. Good connectivity means a strong signal. Word parts: connect (verb) + -ivity (noun ending).
  • productivity (n): how much useful work you finish in a certain time. High productivity = you do a lot of good work efficiently. Word parts: produce → productive (adj) → productivity (noun).
  • slogan (n): a short, catchy phrase that a company repeats to be remembered, like "Just do it".
  • commercial (n or adj): as a noun, a paid advertisement on TV, radio or online. As an adjective, connected with business and making money, as in "a commercial company".
  • memorable (adj): easy to remember or worth remembering. Word parts: memor(y) + -able = able to be remembered.

Why these words belong together

A company makes a commercial. The commercial ends with a short slogan. The company wants the slogan to be memorable so customers remember the brand. Many people watch these commercials on phones, so connectivity matters. At work and school, constant connectivity can help or hurt productivity: you can find information fast, but notifications can also distract you. This story is typical for Part 1 texts about technology and media.

Using word parts to guess

WordPartsMeaning clue
memorablememor(y) + -ablecan be remembered
productivityproduct(ive) + -ityquality of being productive
connectivityconnect + -ivityquality of connecting
Example 1. "The new phone's advert had a short and ____ slogan, so everybody remembered it." Options: (memorable / productive / commercial). After "short and" we need an adjective that matches "remembered it" → memorable.
Example 2. "Many offices install fast Wi-Fi because good ____ improves ____." Gap 1: the quality of connecting → connectivity. Gap 2: the amount of work done → productivity.
Example 3. "The company paid a lot of money to show a thirty-second ____ during the football match." Paid advert on TV → commercial (noun).
⚠️ Common mistakes: (1) Commercial is an advert, not a shop (shop = store). (2) Confusing productivity (noun) with productive (adjective): "a productivity day" is wrong; "a productive day" is right. (3) Using connectivity for a personal link: for people, we say connection or relationship. (4) Spelling: memorable has one "m" in the middle, and slogan has "o" then "a".
Exam corner: These words are tested in Part 1 (Vocabulary), 5 gaps, 2 marks each. They also help in Part 3B (Commercials, Visual Media and language) because the same words appear in the reading text. To answer: find the word class, look for the meaning clue, test each option, and read the whole sentence again.

Quick summary

  • infotainment = information + entertainment.
  • connectivity = ability to connect; productivity = amount of work done.
  • slogan = short catchy phrase; commercial = paid advert; memorable = easy to remember.
  • Use word parts (-ivity, -able) to check the word class.
