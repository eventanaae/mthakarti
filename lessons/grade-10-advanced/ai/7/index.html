Limitations of Neural Networks (حدود الشبكات العصبية)

🤖 الذكاء الاصطناعي والتكنولوجيا🎓 الصف العاشر متقدم📌 هيكل الامتحان: L01 Neural Networks and Deep Learning

The four limitations of neural networks:

LimitationMeaning
1. Lots of data (بيانات كثيرة)They need large amounts of data. Poor data → poor learning.
2. Powerful hardware (أجهزة قوية)Training can be slow and expensive.
3. Black box (الصندوق الأسود)Decisions can be difficult to explain.
4. Bias (التحيّز)Biased data can create unfair outcomes.
• A hospital AI needs thousands of medical images before it predicts accurately → lots of data.
• A bank's AI rejects a loan but the bank cannot explain why → black box.
• An AI trained mostly on one group of people works worse for others → bias.
Starter scenario – facial recognition: a phone does not recognise users in low light, works better for some people than others, and sometimes makes mistakes. Possible reasons: poor lighting, not enough (or not diverse enough) training data, and the AI having difficulty recognising some patterns.
Data quality affects fairness: if training data does not represent everyone, the AI can be unfair to some groups.
⚠️ Neural networks do NOT always explain their decisions and DO need data and computing power.
