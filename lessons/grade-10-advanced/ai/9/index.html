New skills required: critical thinking (التفكير الناقد), data literacy (الثقافة البيانية), AI literacy, ethical reasoning (التفكير الأخلاقي), decision evaluation (تقييم القرارات).
Activity 2.1 – Smart Logistics Hub: AI cameras monitor inventory, autonomous robots move and sort packages, and AI analyses real-time traffic data to optimise delivery routes. Human workers still supervise operations, manage exceptions and make important decisions.
• Shifted to AI: routine monitoring, moving/sorting packages, route planning.
• Still needs humans: unusual problems, safety decisions, final responsibility.
⚠️ If a hub runs with no human supervision, an AI mistake could cause delays or safety issues with no one to catch it – and humans/the organisation remain responsible.
