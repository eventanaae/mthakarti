Smart Metro Operations: AI monitors train speeds in real time, detects possible equipment faults and recommends schedule changes. Human operators monitor the recommendations, investigate unusual situations and make final decisions during emergencies.
Activity 2.4 – UAE bank loan: the AI uses income level, credit history and spending behaviour and recommends "approve". Evaluate with: efficiency, safety (risk), fairness, accountability, human oversight. Ask: could data be missing or biased? A human should check, because the decision affects financial wellbeing. Relying only on AI could cause unfair or harmful decisions that no one can explain.
⚠️ An AI system can be efficient without being safe. Improving speed can reduce safety if humans stop checking.
