1.3 Medical record reports

🩺 العلوم الصحية🎓 الصف الثاني عشر عام📌 هيكل الامتحان: Unit 1 · Lesson 1.3

🎯 ذاكر الدرس وحل الكويز مجانًا في مذاكرتي
A medical record (السجل الطبي) is the official, written history of a patient's health and care. Healthcare professionals use medical terminology and abbreviations to write it quickly and accurately.

A medical record usually contains:

  • personal details (name, date of birth, contact information);
  • medical history – past illnesses, operations, family history;
  • allergies and current medications;
  • signs, symptoms, test results and the diagnosis;
  • the treatment plan and progress notes.

Common abbreviations

AbbreviationMeaning
BPblood pressure

Hxhistory

b.i.d. / t.i.d.twice a day / three times a day
⚠️ Medical records are confidential (سرية): only people involved in the patient's care may read them. They must be accurate, clear, dated and signed.
