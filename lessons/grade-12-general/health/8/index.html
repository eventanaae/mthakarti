1.5 Spelling and pronunciation

🩺 العلوم الصحية🎓 الصف الثاني عشر عام📌 هيكل الامتحان: Unit 1 · Lesson 1.5

🎯 ذاكر الدرس وحل الكويز مجانًا في مذاكرتي
Medical terms are difficult to spell, and some are spelled similarly to other words but have very different meanings, so correct spelling is very important.

Keywords: pronounce – to say a word correctly; syllable (مقطع صوتي) – a single unbroken sound of a spoken word.

  • Pronounce words based on their syllables. The number of syllables depends on the number of vowel sounds.
  • In a word with two or more syllables, one syllable is given more importance – this is word stress.
WordSyllablesCount
gastritisgas-TRI-tis3
arthritisar-THRI-tis3
tonsillitiston-sil-LI-tis4
cardiologycar-di-OL-o-gy5
appendectomyap-pen-DEC-to-my5
Stress patterns: words ending in -itis stress the syllable "-I-"; words ending in -ology stress "OL"; words ending in -ectomy stress "EC".
⚠️ Look-alike words: ileum (part of the small intestine) vs ilium (hip bone). British spellings in the book: haemoglobin, oesophagus, diarrhoea, anaemia. Silent letters: pneumonia, psychology.

What is a syllable?

✅ الجواب: A single unbroken sound of a spoken word

Syllables break a word into sound parts.

How many syllables are in 'cardiology'?

✅ الجواب: 5

car-di-ol-o-gy = 5 syllables.

How many syllables are in 'arthritis'?

✅ الجواب: 3

ar-thri-tis = 3 syllables.
