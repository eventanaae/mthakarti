2.6 Atherosclerosis & other heart and vessel diseases

🩺 العلوم الصحية🎓 الصف الثاني عشر عام📌 هيكل الامتحان: Unit 2 · Lesson 2.6 · Activity 32

🎯 ذاكر الدرس وحل الكويز مجانًا في مذاكرتي
Atherosclerosis (تصلب الشرايين): a fatty substance called plaque builds up in the arteries and eventually blocks blood flow. In the arteries of the heart → heart attack; in the brain → stroke.
  • Risk factors: being overweight, hypertension, not exercising, unhealthy diet with too much salt and fat, family history.
  • Signs: develops very slowly, so people usually do not know until there is a blockage: severe chest pain (blockage in the heart); weakness, loss of vision and difficulty speaking (blockage in the brain); pain in the arms or legs at the blockage.
  • Treatment: lifestyle changes (lose weight, healthy diet, exercise); medication to slow the disease, e.g. cholesterol medication; surgery to remove plaque.
ConditionMeaning
AnginaHeart (chest) pain caused by low blood flow to the heart
Heart failureThe heart is weakened or damaged and cannot pump blood around the body properly
StrokeCaused by poor blood flow or bleeding in the brain
Venous thrombosisA blockage (blood clot) forms in a blood vessel (vein)

Keywords: blockage – an obstruction that makes blood flow difficult or impossible; circulation – movement of blood caused by the pumping of the heart; lifestyle – the way a person lives.

⚠️ Atherosclerosis is the cause (narrowed arteries); a heart attack or stroke is the result when the blockage happens.

اختبر نفسك: أسئلة على 2.6 Atherosclerosis & other heart and vessel diseases

What builds up in the arteries in atherosclerosis?

✅ الجواب: A fatty substance called plaque

Plaque narrows and blocks the arteries.

If atherosclerosis blocks an artery in the brain, it can cause a:

✅ الجواب: Stroke

Blockage in the brain → stroke; in the heart → heart attack.

Why do people often not know they have atherosclerosis?

✅ الجواب: It develops very slowly until there is a blockage

Symptoms appear only when an artery is blocked.
