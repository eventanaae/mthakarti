2.6 Circulatory system & hypertension

🩺 العلوم الصحية🎓 الصف الثاني عشر عام📌 هيكل الامتحان: Unit 2 · Lesson 2.6 · Activities 29, 31

🎯 ذاكر الدرس وحل الكويز مجانًا في مذاكرتي
PartFunction
HeartPumps blood through the body
ArteriesTake blood away from the heart
VeinsTake blood back to the heart
BloodCarries oxygen and nutrients

Conditions: hypertension, atherosclerosis, angina, heart failure, stroke, venous thrombosis.

Hypertension (ارتفاع ضغط الدم) = high blood pressure: the pressure of blood in the arteries is higher than it should be. It is a chronic condition.
  • Risk factors: family history, being overweight, too much salt, high stress, lack of physical activity.
  • Signs: most people have no signs or symptoms; when very high: severe headache, fatigue or confusion, vision problems, chest pain, irregular heartbeat, difficulty breathing.
  • Complications if untreated: heart failure, sight loss, kidney disease, stroke, heart attack.
  • Treatment: regular check-ups (earlier diagnosis = less damage); healthy weight, regular exercise, diet low in salt, fat and caffeine; no smoking; medication that lowers blood pressure and relaxes blood vessels.
A blood pressure monitor reads systolic pressure (when the heart beats) over diastolic pressure (when the heart relaxes). Normal is less than 120/80. A reading above normal (e.g. 140/90) indicates hypertension; a reading well below normal (e.g. 85/55) indicates low blood pressure, hypotension.
⚠️ Hypertension is called a "silent" condition because it usually has no symptoms – that is why regular checks matter.
