2.3 Nervous system & epilepsy

🩺 العلوم الصحية🎓 الصف الثاني عشر عام📌 هيكل الامتحان: Unit 2 · Lesson 2.3 · Activity 16

🎯 ذاكر الدرس وحل الكويز مجانًا في مذاكرتي
The nervous system helps different parts of the body communicate and allows the brain to control what is going on.
Central nervous system (CNS)Peripheral nervous system (PNS)
The brain and the spinal cord; found in the centre of the bodyThe nerves away from the central part of the body, e.g. in the arms and legs

Keywords: degenerative – gets worse over time; seizure (نوبة) – sudden attack with unconsciousness or shaking; tremor (رعشة) – involuntary shaking; dementia – decline in memory, language and thinking.

Conditions: epilepsy, Parkinson's disease, Alzheimer's disease, sciatica.

Epilepsy (الصرع)

  • Abnormal brain activity that leads to seizures; non-communicable and chronic.
  • More common in young children and older adults; cause unknown in most cases. Some causes: brain injury, serious illness, high fever, stroke.
  • Signs of a seizure: stiff body with arching back, noisy difficult breathing, sudden uncontrolled movements, unresponsiveness or unconsciousness, falling down.
  • Treatment: anti-epileptic medication; a diet high in healthy fats and low in carbohydrates; brain surgery to remove the part of the brain causing seizures.
⚠️ The main sign of epilepsy is seizures. The spinal cord is part of the CNS, not the PNS.
