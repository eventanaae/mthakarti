2.5 Diseases of the digestive system

🩺 العلوم الصحية🎓 الصف الثاني عشر عام📌 هيكل الامتحان: Unit 2 · Lesson 2.5 · HSC.5.3.01.005 · Activity 26

The digestive system breaks down food into smaller pieces so it can be turned into nutrients. Parts: mouth (tongue, salivary glands), oesophagus, liver, stomach, pancreas, intestines, colon, rectum.

Gastroenteritis ("stomach flu"): caused by bacteria or viruses, from eating or drinking contaminated food; acute; affects anyone but dangerous for very young children and older adults; most cases communicable (food poisoning is not). Signs: watery diarrhoea, stomach cramps, nausea/vomiting, muscle aches, headache, fever. Treatment: good hygiene, stop solid food for a few hours, rest, stay hydrated; call a doctor straight away for infants/children with a fever of 38.9 °C or more, pain or dehydration.

Food intolerance: common ones are lactose, gluten and eggs; not life-threatening; can affect anyone at any stage of life. Signs: stomach pain, bloating, headache, nausea, diarrhoea. Hard to diagnose because symptoms resemble other conditions – a dietitian asks the patient to keep a food diary, note when they feel ill, then omit suspected foods.

⚠️ Food intolerance is a digestion problem and is not life-threatening; a food allergy is an immune reaction that can be life-threatening.
