Grammar 5: Reported Speech

📗 اللغة الإنجليزية🎓 الصف الثاني عشر عام📌 هيكل الامتحان: Part 2 · G.18 · Career trends text

🎯 ذاكر الدرس وحل الكويز مجانًا في مذاكرتي

We report what someone said by moving the tense one step back (after a past reporting verb such as said, told, explained).

Direct speechReported speech
present simple: "We offer internships."past simple: She said they offered internships.
present continuous: "We are hiring."past continuous: He said they were hiring.
will: "We will offer more."would: She said they would offer more.
can: "I can help."could: He said he could help.
past simple / present perfect: "I lost / have lost my card."past perfect: She said she had lost her card.

today / tonightthat day / that night

yesterday / last monththe day before / the previous month

say (no person): She said (that)… · tell + person: She told me (that)… · Questions: He asked if / whether I had a budget; She asked where I worked (statement word order). Commands: He told me to save / not to spend.
⚠️ ❌ "She said me" → ✔ "She told me" / "She said to me". ❌ "He asked where did I work" → ✔ "He asked where I worked".

The idea in detail

Reported speech (الكلام المنقول / غير المباشر) tells someone what another person said, without quotation marks. Because the words were spoken in the past, we usually backshift (نرجع الزمن خطوة للخلف) the tenses and change pronouns and time words.

Why / how it works

Example from a career article: The manager said, "My company will offer more internships next year."
→ The manager said (that) her company would offer more internships the following year.
Changes: my → her (pronoun), will → would (tense), next year → the following year (time).

Reporting verbs: say takes no person ("He said that…"); tell needs a person ("He told us that…"); explain, add, mention, claim, warn, admit work like say. Questions use ask + if/whether (yes/no) or the wh-word, with normal statement order and no question mark. Advice / commands use tell / advise / ask + person + (not) to + verb.

If the fact is still true, backshift is optional: "She said the UAE is / was a business hub." In the exam, the backshifted form is the safe answer.

Worked example 1

"A company manager said last month that her company ____ more internships the following year." A) will offer B) would offer C) offers → B: said (past) + future → would. "the following year" confirms reported speech.

Worked example 2

Direct: "Do you have a part-time job?" the adviser asked me. → The adviser asked me if I had a part-time job.

Common mistakes

⚠️ 1) Not changing will after a past reporting verb. 2) "said me" → told me. 3) Question order: "asked what was his job" → asked what his job was. 4) Keeping "tomorrow" when the report is days later → the next day.

Exam corner

Clues for reported speech in Part 2: said that, explained that, told, last month… that, and changed time words like the following year, the day before. Pick the backshifted verb: would, could, had + pp, was/were.

Quick summary

  • Backshift: present → past, will → would, can → could, past/present perfect → past perfect.
  • Change pronouns and time words (next year → the following year).
  • say (no person) / tell + person; asked if…; told me to…
