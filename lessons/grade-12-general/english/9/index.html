Grammar 3: Present Continuous Passive

📗 اللغة الإنجليزية🎓 الصف الثاني عشر عام📌 هيكل الامتحان: Part 2 · G.10.3 · Career trends in a changing world

🎯 ذاكر الدرس وحل الكويز مجانًا في مذاكرتي

Form: subject + am / is / are + being + past participle

Active (present continuous)Passive (present continuous)
Digital systems are replacing many routine tasks.Many routine tasks are being replaced by digital systems.
The company is training new staff this month.New staff are being trained this month.
Companies are outsourcing more jobs.More jobs are being outsourced.
Use it for actions in progress now or changing trends around now. Time clues: now, at the moment, currently, at present, these days, nowadays, this year, more and more.
Present simple passivePresent continuous passive
Cars are made in this factory. (general fact)A new model is being made right now. (in progress)
⚠️ Don't forget being: ❌ "Jobs are replaced at present" (possible but loses the in-progress meaning) – the exam expects are being replaced with at present / currently. ❌ "are been replaced" is always wrong.

The idea in detail

The present continuous passive (المبني للمجهول في المضارع المستمر) describes something that is happening to the subject now, or a trend that is developing. It is very common in texts about career trends: "Workers are being encouraged to upgrade their skills."

Why / how it works

Take a present continuous active sentence: "Companies are hiring more temporary workers." The object becomes the subject, and are hiring becomes are being hired: "More temporary workers are being hired." The pattern is always be (am/is/are) + being + past participle.

Compare the three forms of replace:

  • are replacing – active, in progress (subject does it)
  • are replaced – passive, general fact
  • are being replaced – passive, in progress (subject receives it now)

Negative and question: "Jobs aren't being lost." / "Is the office being renovated?"

Worked example 1

"At present, many office tasks done again and again ____ by digital systems." A) replace B) are being replaced C) have replaced
Tasks receive the action → passive. "At present" → in progress. Answer: B.

Worked example 2

Rewrite in the passive: "Universities are offering more online courses this year." → More online courses are being offered (by universities) this year.

Common mistakes

⚠️ 1) "are been replaced" → are being replaced. 2) "is being replace" → is being replaced (past participle). 3) Using it for permanent facts: "Arabic is being spoken in the UAE" → is spoken. 4) Agreement: "Many workers is being trained" → are.

Exam corner

In Part 2 you may see options like replace / are being replaced / have replaced. Two checks: (1) Does the subject do the action or receive it? (2) Is there a time clue for "now / these days"? Receive + now → is/are being + pp.

Quick summary

  • am/is/are + being + past participle.
  • Action in progress or current trend; clues: currently, at present, nowadays.
  • Never "been" here – that is the perfect.
