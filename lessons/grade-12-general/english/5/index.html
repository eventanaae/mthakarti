Nail That Job! Vocabulary B: Skills & Career Trends

Word families: adapt (v) – adaptable (adj) – adaptability (n); communicate (v) – communicative (adj) – communication (n); flexible (adj) – flexibility (n); rigid (adj) – rigidity (n).
⚠️ flexible and rigid are opposites. If the sentence describes something that "cannot change" or "no exceptions", choose rigid.

The idea in detail

The modern workplace changes quickly, so employers look for soft skills. Two key nouns are adaptability (القدرة على التكيّف) and communication (التواصل). Two key adjectives describe jobs and people: flexible (مرن) and rigid (صارم / جامد).

The grammar text in Part 2 is about career trends, so you will also meet: job security (الأمان الوظيفي), outsourcing (الاستعانة بمصادر خارجية), workforce (القوى العاملة), telecommuting (العمل عن بُعد), globalization (العولمة), upgrade (يطوّر / يرفع مستوى) skills, drawback (عيب / سلبية).

Why / how it works

These words form a cause → effect chain (from the course reading on career trends): globalization and technology → companies use outsourcing to hold costs down and there are more temporary jobs → less job security → workers must upgrade their skills and show adaptability. Telecommuting gives flexible hours, but a drawback is that work and personal life can mix, and teamwork may need better communication.

Part of speech is the key: adaptability/communication are nouns ("show adaptability", "communication skills"); flexible/rigid are adjectives ("flexible hours", "rigid rules"). In Part 1 all options are often the same part of speech, so meaning decides.

Worked example 1

"Our manager follows very ____ rules: no one can change their shift, even in an emergency." A) flexible B) rigid C) shortlisted → B: "no one can change" = fixed.

Worked example 2

"When the company moved to a new system, Aisha learned it in two days. Her ____ impressed everyone." A) adaptability B) background C) labor → A: quickly adjusting to something new.

Common mistakes

⚠️ 1) "She is very adaptability" → adaptable (adjective after be). 2) "communications skills" → communication skills. 3) Thinking telecommuting means commuting by train – it means working remotely. 4) A drawback is negative; do not use it for an advantage.

Exam corner

Expect a Part 1 gap testing flexible vs rigid (opposites) or adaptability vs communication (both skills). Clues: change, new situation, adjust → adaptability/flexible; explain, listen, share ideas, teamwork → communication; strict, fixed, cannot change → rigid.

Quick summary

  • adaptability = adjusting to change; communication = sharing ideas clearly.
  • flexible ≠ rigid.
  • Trend words: globalization, outsourcing, workforce, telecommuting, job security, upgrade skills, drawback.
