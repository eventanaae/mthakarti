Course Vocabulary: Banking, Consumerism & Motivation

📗 اللغة الإنجليزية🎓 الصف الثاني عشر عام📌 هيكل الامتحان: L&S pp. 12–19 · R&W pp. 10–14 · Lessons 5–6, 17–20

Checking your bank account

WordMeaning
joint accounta bank account shared by two or more people
balancethe amount of money remaining in an account
deposit (n / v)money put into an account / to put money in
withdrawalmoney taken out of an account
receipta piece of paper listing what you bought and the prices
statementa record of all the money going in and out of an account

Consumerism and the human brain: consumer (a person who buys and uses products), consume (use up), economy, identical (exactly the same), items, access (the chance to get or use something), brand (a company's name for its products), marketing (advertising and selling products).

Does money talk? (motivation): motivate (give someone a reason to act), reward (payment or prize for something you did), pride (pleasure when you do something well), determined (with a strong will), primarily (mostly), independently (on your own).

⚠️ In the statement, each deposit raises the balance and each withdrawal lowers it: 2,000 − 1,300 = 700.

The idea in detail

These words come from the course lessons. They are not on the official Part 1 list, but they appear in the reading texts and help you understand questions.

Banking: a joint account (حساب مشترك) is shared (e.g. by two flatmates). Your balance (الرصيد) is what is left. A deposit (إيداع) goes in; a withdrawal (سحب) comes out. A receipt (إيصال) comes from a shop; a statement (كشف حساب) comes from the bank.

Consumerism: we are all consumers (مستهلكون) because we buy and use products. The world economy (الاقتصاد) depends on consumption. Often two items are identical (متطابقان), yet we choose one brand (علامة تجارية) because of clever marketing (التسويق). Today people have greater access (إمكانية الوصول) to products than ever.

Motivation: does money motivate (يحفّز) us? Research discussed in the course suggests that a money reward (مكافأة) helps with simple tasks, but for creative work people are primarily (بشكل أساسي) motivated by pride (الفخر), purpose and working independently (باستقلالية).

Worked example 1 (reading a statement)

The account starts with two deposits of 1,000 → balance 2,000. Rent of 1,300 is withdrawn → balance 700. A withdrawal of 250 follows → 450. If the flatmates actually paid the movers 215, the bank took 35 too much – that is the "missing money".

Worked example 2

"Two bottles of shampoo are ____: same ingredients, same factory, only the label is different." → identical.

Common mistakes

⚠️ 1) receipt – the p is silent /rɪˈsiːt/. 2) "a statement" from a shop → that is a receipt. 3) consume (verb) vs consumer (person) vs consumerism (the culture of buying). 4) access to (noun + to): "access to products".

Exam corner

Part 3A is about Consumerism and the human brain or Does money talk? Knowing brand, marketing, identical, consume, reward and motivate makes the passage much faster to read.

Quick summary

  • Bank: joint account, balance, deposit ↔ withdrawal, receipt, statement.
  • Consumerism: consumer, consume, brand, marketing, identical, access, economy.
  • Motivation: motivate, reward, pride, determined, primarily, independently.

اختبر نفسك: أسئلة على Course Vocabulary: Banking, Consumerism & Motivation

The amount of money remaining in a bank account is called the ____.

✅ الجواب: balance

Balance = the money left in an account.

An account shared by two or more people is a ____.

✅ الجواب: joint account

Joint account = a shared account.

A record from the bank showing all money going in and out of an account is a ____.

✅ الجواب: statement

A bank statement lists monthly transactions; a receipt comes from a shop.
