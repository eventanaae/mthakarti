Nail That Job! Vocabulary A: Jobs & Applications

WordMeaningExample
career (n)the series of jobs a person has in one area over a working lifeShe hopes to build a long career in medicine.
occupation (n, formal)a job or professionPlease write your name and occupation on the form.
labor (n) (UK: labour)work, especially physical work; also workers as a groupBuilding a road needs a lot of manual labor. / the labor market
internship (n)a short period of work, often for students, to gain experienceHe did a summer internship at a bank.
experience (n)knowledge or skill gained by doing somethingThe job requires two years of work experience.
background (n)a person's education, family, culture and past experienceApplicants come from different educational backgrounds.
shortlisted (adj / past participle)chosen for the final list of candidates, usually for an interviewOnly five applicants were shortlisted for the interview.
Collocations: pursue / build / change a career; career path; manual / skilled labor; do / complete an internship; work experience (uncountable); gain experience; a technical / educational background; be shortlisted for a job.
⚠️ experience (uncountable) = skill/knowledge: "a lot of experience". an experience (countable) = an event: "Travelling alone was an amazing experience."

The idea in detail

The unit Nail That Job! ("nail" = succeed in getting) follows the journey from choosing a path to being invited to an interview.

StageWord

Why / how it works

Career vs occupation vs job: a job is the work you do now; an occupation is the formal name of that type of work (on forms: "Occupation: engineer"); a career is the long path of jobs over many years. If the gap is about the long term or progress ("build", "path", "throughout her life"), choose career.

Labor is uncountable. It means physical or practical work ("manual labor") or the workers in a country ("cheap labor", "the labor market"). American spelling labor is used in the exam list; British is labour.

Shortlisted is often passive: "She was shortlisted for the position." It means you passed the first selection and will probably have an interview.

Worked example 1

"During the summer, Salem completed an ____ with a technology company to gain practical skills." A) background B) internship C) occupation → B: "completed … to gain practical skills" = internship.

Worked example 2

"Out of 200 applicants, only six were ____ for an interview." A) shortlisted B) flexible C) rigid → A.

Common mistakes

⚠️ 1) "many experiences" when you mean work skill → a lot of experience. 2) "a labor" → labor (no article). 3) Using background for a job: background is your past, not your occupation. 4) Confusing career and carrier (a company that carries things).

Exam corner

Signal words: long-term, path, progress → career; form, state your → occupation; manual, physical, cheap, market → labor; summer, student, gain skills → internship; years of, gain, lack of → experience; family, education, different → background; interview, final list → shortlisted.

Quick summary

  • career = long path; occupation = formal job name; labor = work / workers.
  • internship → experience; background = your past.
  • shortlisted = on the final list for interview.
