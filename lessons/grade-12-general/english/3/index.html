Money Talks Vocabulary B: Studies, Business & Persuasion

📗 اللغة الإنجليزية🎓 الصف الثاني عشر عام📌 هيكل الامتحان: Part 1 · Money Talks · student loan, scholarship, raise capital, take risks, risk-taker, influence, persuade

🎯 ذاكر الدرس وحل الكويز مجانًا في مذاكرتي
Word / phraseMeaningExample
student loan (n)money a student borrows to pay for studies and must pay back laterShe took out a student loan to cover her tuition fees.
scholarship (n)money given to a student (often for high grades) that does NOT have to be paid backHe won a government scholarship to study engineering.
raise capitalto collect money to start or grow a businessThe founders raised capital from investors.
take risksto do something even though it may failEntrepreneurs must be willing to take risks.
risk-taker (n)a person who often takes risksAs a risk-taker, he left a safe job to open a café.
influence (v / n)to have an effect on what someone thinks or doesAdverts influence what teenagers buy. / Social media has a strong influence on us.
persuade (v)to make someone agree or do something by giving reasonsThe advert persuaded me to buy the shoes.

Related course words: tuition fees (money paid for a course), alternatively (as another choice), take time off (stop working for a period), entrepreneur, found (start a company), hire (give someone a job), vision (ability to plan for the future).

Patterns: persuade + someone + to do; influence + something (no "on" after the verb); have an influence on (noun + on); take out / repay a student loan; win / receive / apply for a scholarship.
⚠️ A scholarship is free money; a student loan must be repaid. "Influence on" only after the NOUN: "Adverts influence teenagers" (not "influence on teenagers").

The idea in detail

The second half of the Money Talks list is about paying for studies, starting a business and changing people's minds.

GroupWords
Paying for universitystudent loan (قرض طلابي) (repay), scholarship (منحة دراسية) (no repayment), tuition fees (رسوم دراسية)
Starting a businessraise capital (يجمع رأس المال), take risks (يخاطر), risk-taker (شخص مُجازف)
Changing mindsinfluence (يؤثر / تأثير), persuade (يُقنع)

Why / how it works

Loan vs scholarship: both pay for studies, but the key difference is repayment. If the text says "would not have to pay it back" or "for excellent grades", choose scholarship. If it says "borrow", "repay", "interest" or "debt", choose student loan.

Influence vs persuade: influence is general and often hidden ("Music influences our mood"). Persuade means using reasons or arguments to get a result, and it usually takes someone + to-infinitive: "My brother persuaded me to save." Advertisers try to persuade consumers; social pressure influences them.

Take risks vs risk-taker: the first is a verb phrase (what you do), the second a noun (who you are). Entrepreneurs who raise capital must take risks because they may lose the money.

Worked example 1

"Noura's grades were excellent, so she received a ____ that paid all her tuition fees, and she never had to repay anything." A) student loan B) scholarship C) withdrawal → B: no repayment + excellent grades.

Worked example 2

"Before they can buy equipment or hire staff, new business owners usually need to ____." A) make ends meet B) put aside C) raise capital → C: collecting money to start a business.

Common mistakes

⚠️ 1) "persuade me buy" → persuade me to buy. 2) "Adverts influence on young people" → influence young people / have an influence on young people. 3) "a risk-taking" as a noun → a risk-taker. 4) "take a risks" → take risks / take a risk.

Exam corner

How it is asked: gap-fill where two options look similar (loan/scholarship; influence/persuade). Read the sentence after the gap: "...because he would not have to repay it" decides the answer. In reading texts about advertising, persuade and influence appear a lot – know both.

Quick summary

  • student loan = borrow + repay; scholarship = gift for merit.
  • raise capital = gather money for a business; take risks / risk-taker.
  • persuade sb to do sth; influence sb/sth; have an influence on.
