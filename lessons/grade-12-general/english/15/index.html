Reading Skills: Question Types & Strategies

📗 اللغة الإنجليزية🎓 الصف الثاني عشر عام📌 هيكل الامتحان: Parts 3A & 3B · ENG.08.RV.CS learning outcomes

🎯 ذاكر الدرس وحل الكويز مجانًا في مذاكرتي
Question type (spec)Looks like…Strategy
Specific information (3A ×2)"Which payment method…?" "How long…?"Scan for a name, number or key word; read that sentence carefully.
Details (3A ×3, 3B ×3)"According to paragraph 3, why…?"Find the paragraph, then match a paraphrase – the answer rarely uses the same words.
Main point (3A ×1)"What is the main point of paragraph 2?"Read the topic sentence + last sentence; reject options that are only one example.
Inference (3A ×1, 3B ×2)"What can be inferred…?" "…is probably trying to…"Not written directly – choose the option that the clues prove, not one that is merely possible.
Mood / tone / attitude (3B ×1)"How does the writer view…?"Look at adjectives, adverbs and modal verbs (sadly, encouraging, may, must).
Connections (3B ×1)"Which situation fits the writer's position?"Apply the writer's idea to a new real-world example.
Overall meaning (both)"What is the text mainly about / arguing?"Title + introduction + conclusion; the answer covers the whole text.
Tone words: hopeful, optimistic, realistic, cautious, critical, concerned, sceptical, neutral/objective, enthusiastic, disappointed, humorous. Many exam answers combine two: hopeful but realistic.
⚠️ Wrong options are usually: too extreme (always, never, all, only), true but not in the text, in the text but answers a different question, or the opposite of what the text says.

The idea in detail

The test specifications list exactly which comprehension skills are tested. Part 3A (24 marks): 2 × specific information, 3 × details, 1 × main points, 1 × inference, 1 × overall meaning. Part 3B (16 marks): 3 × details, 1 × mood/tone/attitude, 2 × inference, 1 × connections, 1 × overall meaning. If you know the type, you know the strategy.

Why / how it works

Skimming (القراءة السريعة للفكرة العامة) = reading fast for the general idea (first read). Scanning (البحث السريع عن معلومة محددة) = searching for one piece of information (names, numbers). Inference (الاستنتاج) = understanding what the writer suggests but doesn't say. Tone (النبرة / موقف الكاتب) = the writer's feeling or attitude.

Fact vs opinion: facts can be checked ("Countdown clocks show the time left"); opinions use I believe, in my view, should, it is wrong to, clearly. Writers also use questions to involve the reader ("Why do we buy things we don't need?") – often the answer is the main idea of the paragraph.

Reference words: it, they, this, their, such point back to something earlier. "Marketers use their knowledge of psychology" → their = marketers'.

Worked example 1 (inference)

Text: "Before her first salary arrived, Mariam had already listed what she would buy – and what she would save." Question: What can be inferred about Mariam? A) She plans her money carefully. B) She is broke. C) She dislikes shopping. → A. B and C are not supported by any clue.

Worked example 2 (tone)

Text: "Change will not happen overnight, yet small steps are already making a difference." → tone: hopeful but realistic. "will not happen overnight" = realistic; "already making a difference" = hopeful.

Common mistakes

⚠️ 1) Choosing an option because it is true in real life. 2) Choosing the option with the same words as the text (often a trap). 3) For main idea, choosing a detail or an example. 4) Missing NOT/EXCEPT in the question.

Exam corner

Order of work: read the questions first → skim the text (1–2 min) → answer in order (questions usually follow the text order) → for overall meaning, answer last. Underline names, numbers and linkers (however, therefore, although).

Quick summary

  • Know the 7 skill types and their strategies.
  • Paraphrase, not same words; evidence, not guessing.
  • Avoid extreme options (all, never, only).

Which strategy is best for a 'specific information' question (e.g. a number or name)?

✅ الجواب: Scan the text for the key word.

Scanning = searching quickly for one piece of information.

An inference question asks you to find information that is ____.

✅ الجواب: suggested by clues but not stated directly

Inference = what the writer implies; it must be supported by clues.

Which option is MOST LIKELY to be wrong in a reading question?

✅ الجواب: Teenagers always buy what adverts show them.

Extreme words like always, never, all and only often make an option wrong.
