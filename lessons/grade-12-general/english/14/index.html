Part 2 Practice: Grammar in Context (Career Trends)

📗 اللغة الإنجليزية🎓 الصف الثاني عشر عام📌 هيكل الامتحان: Part 2 · Expository text ±170 words · 5 gaps

🎯 ذاكر الدرس وحل الكويز مجانًا في مذاكرتي

Practice text (original, about 170 words): "Work Is Changing – Are You Ready?" (6 gaps for extra practice; the real exam has 5)

Today, the world of work is changing faster than ever. Many routine jobs (1) ____ by robots and smart software at the moment, so young people need to think carefully about their future. Telecommuting, (2) ____ was rare twenty years ago, is now common in many offices. Employers say they want staff who are willing (3) ____ new skills throughout their careers. Last year, a careers adviser told our class that most of us (4) ____ several different jobs during our lives. This may sound worrying. However, workers who upgrade their skills are (5) ____ more likely to find good jobs than those who do not. (6) ____ job security is lower than in the past, there are also more opportunities for people with adaptability and good communication.

GapOptionsAnswer + reason
1are replacing / are being replaced / replacedare being replaced – jobs receive the action; "at the moment" → present continuous passive.
2that / which / whatwhich – non-defining clause (commas) about a thing.
3learning / learn / to learnto learn – be willing + to-infinitive.
4will have / would have / havewould have – reported speech after "told".
5very / much / suchmuch – modifier before a comparative (more likely).
6Despite / Although / UnlessAlthough – full clause + contrast.
Method: (1) read the whole text; (2) find the grammar clue around each gap (comma, time word, reporting verb, comparative, preposition); (3) test each option.

The idea in detail

Part 2 does not ask "What is the present continuous passive?" Instead it hides the grammar inside a short expository text (نص تفسيري / معلوماتي) about career trends. You must recognise which grammar point each gap is testing.

Worked example 1

"The careers fair, ____ is held every March, helps students meet employers." A) where B) which C) who → B: the fair is a thing and it is the subject of "is held" (where would need a full clause: "where students meet…").

Worked example 2

"Employees can't work from home ____ their manager agrees." A) unless B) although C) whereas → A: = if their manager doesn't agree, they can't.

Common mistakes

⚠️ 1) Choosing by "sound" instead of the clue. 2) Choosing where after any place-like noun – check if a subject is missing (then use which). 3) Forgetting reported speech when the sentence starts with "Last year … said".

Exam corner

5 gaps × 2 marks. The real text is about Nail That Job! – Career trends in a changing world, so expect words like telecommuting, outsourcing, job security, workforce, upgrade, drawback around the gaps.

Quick summary

  • Identify the grammar point from the clue, then choose.
  • Seven points: verb forms, 2 passives, non-defining clauses, reported speech, degree adverbs, subordinating conjunctions.

اختبر نفسك: أسئلة على Part 2 Practice: Grammar in Context (Career Trends)

'At the moment, many routine jobs ____ by robots.' Which grammar point does this gap test?

✅ الجواب: present continuous passive

Thing as subject + 'at the moment' → is/are being + past participle.

Telecommuting, ____ was rare twenty years ago, is now common in many offices.

✅ الجواب: which

Comma + thing + missing subject → which.

Last year, a careers adviser told our class that most of us ____ several different jobs during our lives.

✅ الجواب: would have

Reported speech after 'told' → will becomes would.
