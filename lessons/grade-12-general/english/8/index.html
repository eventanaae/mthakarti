Grammar 2: Present Simple Passive

📗 اللغة الإنجليزية🎓 الصف الثاني عشر عام📌 هيكل الامتحان: Part 2 · G.10.1 · R&W pp. 10–14 (Consumerism and the Human Brain)

🎯 ذاكر الدرس وحل الكويز مجانًا في مذاكرتي

Form: subject + am / is / are + past participle (+ by + agent)

ActivePassive
Advertisers persuade consumers.Consumers are persuaded by advertisers.
Marketers use psychology to influence buyers.Psychology is used to influence buyers.
Shops spray floral scents.Floral scents are sprayed in shops.
Companies do not test every product.Not every product is tested.
Use the passive when the action or the receiver is more important than the doer, or when the doer is unknown or obvious: "The world economy is based on consumerism." Questions: "Is the product made in the UAE?"
⚠️ Use the past participle, not the past simple: is written (not wrote), are chosen (not chose), is paid (not payed). The verb be agrees with the subject: "Two identical products are packaged…".

The idea in detail

In an active sentence the subject does the action: "Advertisers persuade consumers." In a passive (المبني للمجهول) sentence the subject receives the action: "Consumers are persuaded by advertisers." The present simple passive describes facts, habits and general truths.

Why / how it works

Step 1 – take the object of the active sentence (consumers) and make it the subject. Step 2 – choose am/is/are to agree with the new subject. Step 3 – add the past participle (التصريف الثالث) of the main verb (persuade → persuaded). Step 4 – add by + agent only if it is useful.

We use the passive in expository texts (like the exam texts) because they focus on processes and results: "Products are advertised to children", "Many items are bought on impulse", "Workers are paid monthly".

Pronunciation: in "ConSUmers are perSUAded by ADvertisers" the main words are stressed and are and by are weak.

Worked example 1

"In many TV adverts, SUVs ____ (show) driving up rocky mountains." → are shown (plural subject SUVs; show → shown).

Worked example 2

"Many jobs ____ in different countries when companies send work abroad." A) carry out B) carried out C) are carried out → C: jobs don't carry out anything – they are done by someone.

Common mistakes

⚠️ 1) Forgetting be: "Consumers persuaded by adverts" → are persuaded. 2) Wrong agreement: "The products is sold" → are sold. 3) Past simple instead of participle: "is buyed / is bought" → is bought. 4) Using a passive with an intransitive verb: ❌ "Prices are risen" → ✔ "Prices rise".

Exam corner

If the subject before the gap is a thing that cannot do the action (products, tasks, jobs, money), expect a passive. Then decide the tense: general fact → is/are + pp; happening now / these days → is/are being + pp.

Quick summary

  • am/is/are + past participle (+ by agent).
  • Focus on the receiver or the process; doer unknown or unimportant.
  • Agreement and past participle forms are the usual traps.
