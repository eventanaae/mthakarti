Data governance, accountability & risks

🤖 الذكاء الاصطناعي والتكنولوجيا🎓 الصف الثاني عشر عام📌 هيكل الامتحان: AI L04 · slides 20–27

🎯 ذاكر الدرس وحل الكويز مجانًا في مذاكرتي
Data governance (حوكمة البيانات) = a framework of rules, policies and processes that manages how data is collected, stored, accessed, used and shared in an organisation. Trustworthy AI depends on well-managed data.
ElementMeaning
Data qualityData is accurate, complete and up to date before training or running AI
Data access controlOnly authorised people and systems can access sensitive data
Data transparencyBe open about what data is collected, how it is used and who it is shared with
Data accountabilitySomeone is responsible – e.g. a Data Protection Officer (DPO)
Data privacyPersonal information protected by privacy laws; used only for stated purposes
PracticeHow it creates accountabilityExample
Clear data ownershipA named person/team is responsibleA DPO ensures AI data complies with privacy, consent and security rules
Audit trails (سجلات التدقيق)Every action on data is logged for later reviewA log shows which staff member accessed patient data and when
Consent and purpose limitationData used only for the purpose people agreed toMedical data collected for treatment cannot be sold
Data quality checksAccurate data makes AI decisions more secureA hiring AI is held accountable when CV data errors cause unfair rejections

Risks of poor data governance in large-scale AI:

RiskExample
1 Biased outputsFace recognition trained on one group performs poorly on others
2 Privacy violationsPatient data used for commercial research without knowledge
3 Loss of accountabilityUnfair loan rejections but no audit trail to investigate
4 Destruction of public trustMisused surveillance data makes people withdraw from the system
5 Data poisoning (تسميم البيانات)Attackers add fake medical records so the AI learns wrong patterns
6 Regulatory and legal consequencesA company collecting data without consent is fined
7 Cascading errorsIncorrect customer data → thousands of loan applications assessed wrongly
8 Lack of transparencyNo records, so the company cannot explain why an applicant was rejected
⚠️ Data poisoning is deliberate (attackers add false data); cascading errors start from an accidental small error that spreads.

Why does scale matter? In a system used by millions, one biased pattern or data error is repeated millions of times – harming many people at once and causing bigger legal and reputation damage than in a small local system.

Activity 4.3 – smart city traffic AI (cameras, road sensors, mobile apps): location data from phones and camera images of people need protection. Improve trust by explaining what data is collected, getting consent, limiting access, checking data quality and publishing how decisions are made.

Activity 4.4 – surgery prioritisation AI: issues – (1) some patient groups get lower priority with similar conditions → biased data; (2) doctors cannot understand recommendations → lack of transparency; (3) hospitals collect and store data differently and some departments used data without documentation → poor data quality and loss of accountability. Actions: audit and rebalance the data, add explainability tools, set common data standards with a named data owner (DPO) and audit trails.

Activity 4.5 – e-commerce, 30 million users: data from mostly one demographic, never cleaned, no retention or access policy → biased recommendations, privacy risks, legal fines. Introduce data quality checks and access-control/retention policies.
