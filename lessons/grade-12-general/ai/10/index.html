AI knowledge = understanding the concepts, principles and implications of AI: What is AI? How does it work? How does it affect the world? AI skills are interdisciplinary competencies to understand and apply AI confidently, think critically about its risks and opportunities, and use it effectively at work.

High-demand skills for the near future: Technical – programming (Python), data skills, math and statistics, machine learning basics. Non-technical – critical thinking (checking AI output), prompt engineering (هندسة الأوامر) (asking AI clear, effective questions), AI ethics, communication and collaboration, adaptability.

AI in higher education: medicine (diagnosing diseases, analysing medical images, predicting outcomes) · engineering (smart systems, automation) · business (market analysis, financial forecasting, customer service automation). Many UAE universities offer degrees with an AI concentration.

AI toolUse in study and career
CV filters and career assistanceScreen and filter CVs in recruitment; help build CVs for the UAE job market
Recommendation systemsSuggest courses, universities, career paths and jobs
LLMs (Large Language Models)Help with writing, research and studying in Arabic and English
Connections: the UAE National Strategy for Artificial Intelligence 2031 and Centennial 2071 support an AI-driven economy; AI skills prepare students for high-tech jobs, smart cities, digital services, green solutions, research and entrepreneurship.
⚠️ Prompt engineering and critical thinking are listed as non-technical skills; data literacy and programming are technical.

Activity 3.6 – ATS-friendly CV. An Applicant Tracking System (ATS) is software companies use to screen and organise job applications before humans review them. An ATS-friendly CV uses clear headings, simple formatting and relevant keywords. Include: personal details, career objective/summary, education, skills, certifications/achievements, projects or extracurricular activities.
