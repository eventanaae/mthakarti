AI in environmental analysis & evaluating AI solutions

🤖 الذكاء الاصطناعي والتكنولوجيا🎓 الصف الثاني عشر عام📌 هيكل الامتحان: AI L01 · slides 26–39

🎯 ذاكر الدرس وحل الكويز مجانًا في مذاكرتي

AI helps collect, analyse and interpret environmental data to monitor changes, identify patterns and predict natural hazards:

  • Monitoring (الرصد) – tracks rainfall, river levels, temperature and satellite images.
  • Modelling (النمذجة) – finds patterns and relationships in data to build models of environmental systems.
  • Prediction (التنبؤ) – forecasts floods, wildfires and droughts before they occur.
UAE example: the National Centre of Meteorology (NCM) launched two AI-powered assistants – Al Rasid and Forecaster Assistant – to support meteorologists with faster data analysis, better forecasts and more accurate early warnings.

Activity 1.3 – how AI flood forecasting works. A typical system is trained on historical and live data: rainfall, river levels, terrain/elevation, soil moisture, weather forecasts and satellite images. Machine-learning models learn patterns that came before past floods, are trained on old data and tested on data they have not seen. The system predicts where and when water levels will rise, and warnings are shared with communities and emergency teams (alerts, apps, maps). Accuracy depends on data quality, sensor coverage and unusual extreme events.

Activity 1.5 – image classification for disaster photos.

  1. Collect data: three classes – No visible damage, Minor damage (cracks, broken windows), Severe damage (collapsed structures, debris).
  2. Train an image classification model on the three classes.
  3. Test with clear, blurry/poor-quality images and different lighting and angles; record correct and incorrect predictions.
  4. Evaluate: which class was hardest? (Often minor damage, because it sits between the other two.) Wrong classifications could send help to the wrong place, so the AI should be used with human verification.

Activity 1.6 – Smart AI Emergency App prototype (health emergency, earthquake rescue or flood response): users type an emergency message, the app shows a fixed location and a safety tip "from an AI assistant". Think about who benefits and who may need extra support (elderly people, people without smartphones).

Exit task ideas: human oversight is still needed because AI can be wrong or biased; poor data quality gives poor results ("garbage in, garbage out"); developers and users share responsibility for safe use.
