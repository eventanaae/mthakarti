Workforce readiness, lifelong learning & AI in the UAE

🤖 الذكاء الاصطناعي والتكنولوجيا🎓 الصف الثاني عشر عام📌 هيكل الامتحان: AI L02 · slides 24–36

🎯 ذاكر الدرس وحل الكويز مجانًا في مذاكرتي
Key wordMeaning
Upskilling (رفع المهارات)Learning new skills or improving existing ones to keep up with workplace changes
Lifelong learning (التعلم مدى الحياة)Continuous learning throughout life to adapt to changing needs
Workforce readinessDeveloping the knowledge and skills needed to adapt to changing job requirements

As AI becomes common, workers may need to learn new digital tools, work alongside AI systems, update their skills regularly and adapt to changing job requirements.

Workforce readiness skills: digital literacy · adaptability · problem-solving · collaboration · communication · analytical thinking.

Workers may need to upskill and reskill in AI literacy, data analysis, digital technologies and software development. Upskilling includes learning new technologies, updating technical skills, developing leadership and strengthening analytical thinking. Organisations also have a responsibility to invest in training.

Lifelong learning can include courses and certifications, workplace training, independent research and professional development programmes.

UAE Strategy for Artificial Intelligence: launched in 2017 – the UAE was one of the first countries with a national AI strategy. It supports UAE Centennial 2071, aims to build an integrated smart digital system and make the UAE a global leader in AI investment and innovation.
Five themes: 1) forming the UAE AI Council; 2) workshops, programmes, initiatives and field visits to government bodies; 3) developing staff skills and training government officials; 4) providing all services via AI and fully integrating AI into medical and security services; 5) launching a leadership strategy and a government law on the safe use of AI.
UAE sectorHow AI helps
Renewable energyHelps manage renewable energy facilities (e.g. solar farms, smart grids)
Water managementAnalyses data and conducts studies to provide water sources
Transport and trafficReduces accidents and traffic jams and helps draw more effective traffic policies
⚠️ Upskilling = improving skills for your current/changing job; lifelong learning = learning continuously throughout your whole life.

Why do workers who stop learning face challenges? AI tools change quickly; someone who does not update their skills may not be able to use new systems, may be left with only routine tasks that are being automated, and may lose job opportunities.

Skills that complement AI (hard for AI to copy):

  • Creativity – generating new ideas and innovative solutions.
  • Complex problem-solving – analysing difficult problems and developing effective solutions.
  • Critical thinking – evaluating information objectively and making informed decisions.
  • Emotional intelligence – understanding and managing emotions and working well with others.

Activity 2.3 – skills plan: choose a career, name three skills that AI makes more important (e.g. data literacy, critical thinking, adaptability) and how to develop them (online courses, projects, internships).

Activity 2.4/2.5: UAE case study (government: AI services, virtual assistants, smart city operations; energy: AI in solar farms, smart electricity grids) and designing an AI app interface with a prompt-based AI UI design tool – include purpose, target users, key AI features and main screens; then evaluate job impact and privacy, security and ethical issues.
